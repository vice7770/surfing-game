# Viscosity/update fusion: exact parity, no adoption

The candidate removes one adjacent dispatch per CFL substep while retaining the original F32 viscosity stores and update storage reads. Its six small GPU cases and 24 balanced complete-device-step pairs passed exact nine-field parity. The paired median saving was only **0.10 ms**, with order sensitivity and a large baseline outlier; the fusion was **not adopted**. No ordinary FPS or visual trial followed.

| Complete `.step` measure, ms | Original | Fused |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Median total | 5.30 | 5.10 |
| p95, nearest rank | 5.80 | 5.60 |
| Maximum | 26.90 | 10.30 |

Paired median saving is 0.10 ms (the 0.20 ms difference between marginal medians is a different statistic). 18/24 pairs were faster, 6 slower. Paired mean 0.875 ms is dominated by the baseline outlier. Original-first mean saving was 0.142 ms, fused-first 1.608 ms; corresponding paired medians 0.05/0.25 ms. These quantized, short, synthetic measurements do not justify ordinary FPS acceptance.

The cost fixture was supplied 160 × 725 = 116,000-cell nonuniform water with 64 seeded sea components, offshore relaxation and side feed, not a historical or ordinary Padang state. Four paired warm steps preceded 24 alternating orders. Both arms used three CFL substeps in every timed pair at 1/60 s; a per-one-substep saving was not measured. Each timed await used the unchanged `.step`, including packing, CFL scan, encode/submit, map/readback and unpack/adopt. Host creation, extra viscosity reads, parity scans and statistics were outside that duration. No GPU timestamp, profiler, draw workload or late-state test was used. Raw rows and the descriptive [summary](summary.json) retain component timings and complete limitations.

Parity covered open/wall breaking and open breaking-disabled water, each at one/multiple CFL substeps, three consecutive steps (18 paired steps). All nine mapped fields—H, QX, QZ, RATEH, STRENGTH, AGE, NU, PREDX, PREDZ—were finite and F32-bit identical; clocks/substep counts matched. The 65 × 19 inputs had 1,098 wet/137 dry cells, flows, predictors and plunge holds. First-step VISCX/Z bytes matched, with nonzero terms for breaking and zero NU/VISC while water still changed for breaking-disabled cases. Periodic device steps remain refused by the original host; no periodic GPU claim is made.

The [original numeric diff](source/original-fusion.patch) changes only two GPU files from accepted Git 6f321d704269f9f1afc73750ab2b1f4a86f61122. `viscous` reads settled neighbour NU/QX/QZ/SHEAR and writes only local VISC; `update` reads that local output and writes H/PBAR/QBAR, none read by viscosity. Original bodies/stores/reloads stay literal in separate helpers, so a helper-local return cannot skip the subsequent update call. Original diagnostic entry points remain compiled; only default dispatch sequence 17 → 16 changes. No submission/readback batching or field-layout change occurs.
