## Concrete acceptance criteria derived from these observations

1. **Coherent curl:** exterior/side views show a smooth steep face curling into a rounded lip and roof. No metre-scale fins, self-folding walls, detached-looking slabs, sudden cross-front steps or white/sandy cut wedges dominate the silhouette.
2. **Readable hollow mouth:** a mature opening is visibly continuous with the surrounding wave, with an actual gap beneath the roof and an unobstructed direction along the tube. A broad canopy, flat shelf or tiny curl alone does not satisfy this.
3. **Interior view:** inside the air pocket, a dark/green curved roof and wall surround a bright exit ahead. The view must not become underwater sand/fog while the camera is in the observed dry cavity. Camera placement alone cannot prove rider passage.
4. **Motion and joins:** a moving capture retains the smooth roof/face join as the lip throws, peels, lands and closes. The roof must not teleport, shear into fins, abruptly melt into slits or leave detached strips during handoff to the ordinary water/whitewater.
5. **Effects preserve shape:** spray and foam gather at the lip, landing and rider contact. They may be stylized, but must not blanket the opening or hide malformed geometry.
6. **Rideable scale and passage:** an ordinary rider can reach the pocket, crouch under the drawn roof with matching physical clearance, travel inside for an observable interval and leave through the exit. Standing outside for a few seconds, a static placement, or an empty-cavity mesh check is supporting evidence only.

These are qualitative requirements for the user's existing goal. They add no invented numeric duration, precision-shading demand, or FPS work. Suggested proof is a normal outside-to-inside ride capture plus fixed exterior/side and inside inspection views of that same mature section, spanning formation through collapse. Geometry/body evidence should explain what the movie shows, not replace it.
