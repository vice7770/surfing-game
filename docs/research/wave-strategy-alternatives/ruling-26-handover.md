Tags: [M] measured in `t5-sea-side.md`; [R] reasoned; [P] provisional.

## What the probe found

- **2 to 4 s before the throw, the live sea's wave is about 17 % lower than the case's wave as the game places it** [M]. Median ratio 0.83, 0.82, 0.84 at 2, 3, 4 s. The bar was 0.90 to 1.10. It fails.
- **The crests stand in the same place** (within 0.1 to 1.3 m) [M]. Height and slope differ, not place.
- **Most of the gap is the trough ahead:** the sea's trough is 0.10 to 0.17 m less deep. The sea's crest is only 0.06 to 0.09 m lower, and its face is 4 to 7° flatter [M].
- **On the long reach (s 101 to 151 m) the sea is lower still:** ratio 0.62 to 0.76 [M].
- **The earlier "1.4 times" was mostly a mix of units.** The game places each slice at h0 = foot crest / A0, about 3.45 m, not at the run's 4.5 m. At the game's size the case's wave 2 to 4 s out is 1.6 to 1.8 m, not 2.2 to 2.4 m [M].

## What it means

- **This is the live sea's known limit near breaking.** It cannot steepen and grow like real water in the last seconds (its face holds about 15 to 20°). The Basilisk case is the reference physics. So the sea is the one that is short here [R]. This is the reason C2 exists, not a reason against it.
- Part of the trough gap may be the sea's set-up: in a steady train of waves, the mean water level rises near the break. The two-crest case has not built one yet [R]. The coarse sea under C2's zone will keep the set-up. So place the case on the local mean level, not on still water [P].

## Decision for the handover

1. **Read the gap further out when T5's level-13 data lands.** T5's library reaches 5.4 s before the throw. Print the same table at Δ = 4.5, 5 and 5.4 s, case side from the level-13 library (not the level-10 scout).
2. **If the median ratio is 0.90 or more at 5 s:** hand over at about 5 s. The library then leads the wave before the sea's error grows.
3. **If it is still under 0.90:** pick each slice's case from the sea's own wave at the handover (its H and period), not from the foot crest. The library wave then grows from the real incoming wave by its own physics. The break gets about 0.83 times smaller unless the incoming swell is larger. Choosing the swell is then a design question for the owner (the 2 m pool wave), not a bar to tune.
4. **In both cases:** blend height and water speed over at least 1 s at the handover, and place the case on the local mean level. The handover bars promised to the owner stand: the water under a board may not jump more than a few cm, its speed may not change suddenly, and the surface may not rise under a sitting board faster than about 0.4 m/s.
5. **The long reach (s 101 to 151 m):** find out why the sea's wave is lower there before C2 uses that stretch.
