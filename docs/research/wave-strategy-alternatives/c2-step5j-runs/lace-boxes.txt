What they say: the aged lace is NOT removed by ?foamAlbedo=bubbles, and it is not darkened either -- it comes
back UP, from on's 205.1 of 255 to 208.8 against the off leg's 222.9 on bore-face, while its 8 px network
flattens from 1.89 to 1.41 against 1.99. The mechanism is in the composition: the foam white is mixed over
the water by the COVER, so once the foam's albedo falls below the lit water's own brightness, taking it lower
makes the pixel BRIGHTER, not darker. The aged end has a floor, and it is the water under it.
