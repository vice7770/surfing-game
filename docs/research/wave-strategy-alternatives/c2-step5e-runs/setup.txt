  npx rolldown docs/research/wave-strategy-alternatives/c2-step5e-face.ts -o <scratch>/s5e.mjs --format esm --platform node

pool-flow-and-cover.txt  node <scratch>/s5e.mjs --parts RD --cache <scratch>/sea5e.bin --from 40 --until 62

The two pool files are independent windows of the same sea (40-100 s and 40-62 s); the shorter one also carries the
water speeds along the face and the share of the face under the loft's ceiling, which the probe gained after the
longer run had started. Their angles agree to 0.2 degrees, which is the replication.

The sea cache (<scratch>/sea5e.bin) is the spun-up 2 m pool at ?poolSize=ref with the owner's ride switches; it is not
checked in (12 MB). Delete it to rebuild, which takes about 25 minutes on the CPU.

Verified before anything was quoted: `--dump` prints a face point by point. The first pass of this round windowed on
FieldSlice.s (the crest's place at the hand-over, tens of metres behind the wave) and read 2.3-degree faces off the
flat platform; the dump is why that did not reach the write-up.
