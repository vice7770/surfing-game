Beside it, today's drawn body: every stance simulated by the real rider (`src/dev/ridingPoses.ts`) and drawn as the game draws it (every step through the motion estimate and the smoothing layer, so a stance read mid-blend shows the blend) on each of the four surfers' own skeletons, Regular and Goofy, then measured by the stance gauge (`src/scene/rig/stanceGauge.ts`). "Today" is the mean over the four surfers, with their spread; a target is met when the mean of all eight readings is inside it. For the measures the physics' seven points set, their own reading is given too.

## How to read it

- **Provenance (the user's rule, Q20):** peer-reviewed measurements first, then the thesis (de Sousa 2022), then coaching cues, then the approved videos as read from their frames. A target claims at most measured → high, thesis → medium, coaching → medium, video → low. A coaching cue turned into numbers, or a direction read as a range, is marked in its note.
- **Gaps:** where no source reaches a measure there is no target. It is listed under the stance, never filled from the drawn body.
- **Who owns a gap:** the physics' posture (step 6) when its own points miss the target too, for what they set (the trunk, the lean, the hands' height, the feet, the weight); otherwise the drawn pose (step 3): the rig's own choices, how it maps the physics' crouch onto the legs, and anything it moves away from the points. The physics changes only where the map proves it wrong.
- **Pictures:** cited by link and timestamp below; nothing is copied into the repo. The surfer sheet's `?stances` view draws each stance beside a reference figure built from these targets.

## Measures

On the board: x across, y the deck's normal, z the nose. Regular faces −x (the toes' rail) with the left foot forward; Goofy faces +x, the right foot forward. The lead arm is the front foot's side.

- **Knees, hips, elbows:** included angles, 180° straight. The hip is between the trunk (the spine's base to the neck) and the thigh, so it includes the lower back.
- **Ankles:** the shin's tilt from the deck's normal (the ankle's flexion with the foot flat).
- **Trunk:** its tilt from the deck's normal, toward the toes and toward the nose; and from the world's vertical toward the toes (**trunk from the vertical**, as a picture shows it, the lean into a turn included). **Lean:** the feet's middle to the neck, from the world's vertical toward the toes.
- **Twists and head yaw:** where the chest, the pelvis and the head face, on the deck: 0° toward the toes, + toward the nose. **Head pitch:** below the deck's plane.
- **Arms:** the upper arm from the trunk's down: 0° hanging, 90° out, 180° overhead.
- **Lower hand:** the lower wrist's height above the board's centre (about the waterline). **Stance width:** the ankles apart along the stringer. **Weight:** the hips' centre between the rear ankle (0) and the front ankle (1), standing in for the share of weight on the front foot.
