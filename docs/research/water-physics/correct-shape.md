# The correct breaking-wave shape

This is the reference for checking any breaking-wave shape. It gives each stage's correct shape, its sourced numbers and where the game departs today. H is the breaking wave's height. Sessions can consult it by messaging "Water physics research".

## Four stages of a plunging break

*A four-stage schematic of a plunging break (vertical face, throw, touchdown, collapse) is drawn in the Claude Doc version of this page.*

The game's solver cannot draw stages 1–3. Its face stays near 17°, so the wall under the lip never forms, and the tube has to come from a shape added on top.

## Checklist: correct shape against the game today

| Stage | Correct shape and numbers | Game today |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Onset | The crest face goes vertical as crest water reaches 0.85–0.88 of the crest's speed, and the lip throws at 1.0 ([Derakhti 2020](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.06896)). Plungers break at H/h 0.7–0.8, spillers at 0.6–0.7 ([Carini 2019](https://digital.lib.washington.edu/researchworks/items/62cc24f5-5f09-43b5-9a42-ced8a78187b8)) | Breaking starts on the surface's rise rate. Each 1 m column throws once; the face stays near 17–27° |
| Throw | The lip leaves at 1.1–1.3× crest speed and falls close to free fall ([Erinin 2023](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-fluid-mechanics/article/plunging-breakers-part-1-analysis-of-an-ensemble-of-wave-profiles/9DA630A5718361A3579618BB022B8F35), [Drazen 2008](https://airsea.ucsd.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/10/2019/06/2008_Drazen_Melville_Lenain-Journal_of_Fluid_Mechanics_vol_611.pdf)) | 1.2–1.6× crest speed: on the fast side |
| Lip | Jet area 0.012–0.27 H², growing fast with bed slope. Mean thickness is about 0.05 H on 1:50, 0.12 H on 1:20 and 0.2–0.27 H on 1:15–1:10 ([Pick & Feddersen 2026](https://falk.ucsd.edu/pdf/Pick2026JFM_ScalingWaveOverturning.pdf)) | Same fits for the water, but drawn as a separate 8-parcel sheet |
| Tube | Its back wall is the vertical face. Void 0.04–0.43 H², width/length 0.26–0.51 in the field, up to 0.67–0.70 on reefs, tilted 22–53° ([Feddersen 2023](https://falk.ucsd.edu/pdf/Feddersen2023JFM_WindEffectsWaveShape.pdf), [O'Dea 2021](https://www2.whoi.edu/staff/elgar/wp-content/uploads/sites/153/2021/08/145.pdf), [Mead & Black 2001](http://joas.free.fr/studies/bei/g2s/predicting_the_breaking_waves_intensity.pdf)). Offshore wind doubles the area | Void from the fits (width/length up to about 0.45), lower half only. Its back wall is the slope |
| Along the crest | An open curl over about 2.6 H/tan α of crest (3–14 m for 2–3 m waves at peel angles of 30–60°), and behind it a closed void that reshapes within 0.5 s ([O'Dea 2021](https://www2.whoi.edu/staff/elgar/wp-content/uploads/sites/153/2021/08/145.pdf); lengths inferred from peel speed) | Each 1 m column runs on its own clock, which gives teeth |
| After | Roller about 2.9 H long, its face easing from 25° to 16–22°, density about 0.87 of water ([Martins 2018](https://purehost.bath.ac.uk/ws/files/170060912/Martins_et_al_JGR2018.pdf)). Fresh foam reflects about 41 %, fading to 16 % by 10 s ([Koepke 1984](https://user.eumetsat.int/s3/eup-strapi-media/pdf_il_07_07_13_a_dfa14e9e2f.pdf)) | No roller geometry; foam is flat lace. The Reef's foam fades fastest of any spot |
