# Along the crest: how a barrel peels

Along a steady peel, every point of the crest holds the same 2D overturn at a different age. The shoulder, mouth, throat, pit and collapsing tube are stages of one wave, set by a single clock that runs down the line with the peel. At Surf Ranch, lidar shows 0.9 m of crest equals 0.28 s of breaking ([Feddersen et al. 2023](https://falk.ucsd.edu/pdf/Feddersen2023JFM_WindEffectsWaveShape.pdf)).

## The stages, down the line

| Part | Age: time since the face went vertical (T = time to touchdown, about 0.6 √H s) | What it looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shoulder | Before 0 | The face steepening |
| Mouth | About 0.3 T | The lip just launching. The opening's outline is the lip tip's falling path |
| Throat | Up to T | The lip in flight, open underneath |
| Pit | T | Touchdown: the roundest, steepest ring |
| Collapsing tube | T to T + 0.5 s | The ring stretches to length/width 5 and fills with splash-up ([O'Dea et al. 2021](https://www2.whoi.edu/staff/elgar/wp-content/uploads/sites/153/2021/08/145.pdf)) |

## Numbers to hit

- **Open tube along the crest:** about 2.6 H/tan α (range 1.3–3.5 H/tan α), where α is the peel angle. That is 3–14 m for 2–3 m waves at 30–60°. At Surf Ranch it gives 2.7–3.6 m, matching the lidar. This is inferred from crest speed ≈ 4.4 √H and T.
- **The clock:** the stage changes by tan α/c seconds per metre of crest, 0.31 s/m at Surf Ranch. Neighbouring slices stay within one library stage.
- **Makeable barrels:** a rider must travel at c/sin α. At 2–3 m that is 8.8–10.8 m/s at 45° and 12.4–15.2 m/s at 30°. GPS-tracked surfers peak at 9.3 m/s on average and 12.5 m/s at most ([Farley et al. 2012](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2012/07/120723094818.htm)). So sections peeling at under about 35–45° are made only through the tube, or close out. The game's Reef, at a 23° median, needs 13.6–14.8 m/s, true to "made only through the tube".
- **Closing sections:** where the peel angle drops, the clock goes flat and the lips land together. Trapped air then bursts out of the tube's open end as spit, along the tube.
- **The lip:** the tip is about 0.07–0.08 H thick, from the Surf Ranch simulation ([Feddersen et al. 2024](https://falk.ucsd.edu/pdf/Feddersen_etal2024JFM_revised.pdf)). The root is about 0.10–0.21 H, from jet area, so the lip tapers 2–3:1. Teahupo'o's "half the wave height" is expert commentary, and describes the root at most.
- **Each slice feels its own slope:** the bed slope along its path is the contour slope × cos α. At Surf Ranch that gives 0.067, against the simulation's 0.069. Faster, lower-angle sections feel steeper beds, so they are rounder and heavier.
