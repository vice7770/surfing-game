# Padang Padang: what it would take

Padang Padang is a left-hand reef break over a shallow, sharp coral reef, not a beach break. Its barrel is in Mead & Black's "very hollow" class. The game could size that tube honestly today, but it needs its own reef, and the barrel work, to look like the footage.

## What the break is

- **Reef:** very shallow, very sharp coral. The swell arrives from deep water and meets it abruptly. A deep channel with a strong rip ends the ride ([surf-camp guide](https://www.balisurfingcamp.com/surf-spots/uluwatu-area/padang-padang-lefts)).
- **Swell and wind:** south-west groundswell, with periods often over 16 s, that reaches the reef only by wrapping around the Bukit's western tip. It works from about 4 ft and best at 6–10 ft ([Mondo Surf](https://www.mondo.surf/surf-spot/padang-padang/guide/11668)). The south-east trade wind blows offshore in the dry season, April–October. Low tide gives the biggest top-to-bottom barrels.
- **Ride:** a left of 50–150 m, with two to four barrel sections.
- **Measured tube:** Mead & Black fitted three Padang Padang photos ([Mead & Black 2001](http://joas.free.fr/studies/bei/g2s/predicting_the_breaking_waves_intensity.pdf)).
  - The tube is 1.97–2.14 times as long as it is wide, which is their "very hollow" class, shared with Pipeline's Backdoor. Pipeline and Shark Island are one class up ("square, spitting").
  - It is about 0.8 H long and 0.4 H wide, tilted 29–41°.
  - Inverting their fit puts the reef's slope along the wave's path near 1:18–1:20. No survey was found to confirm it.

## How it compares with the game's Reef

|  | Padang Padang | The game's Reef (Teahupo'o) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wave | A left over a shallow coral shelf, into a channel | A left over a coral ledge, toward a deep pass |
| Slope along the wave's path | About 1:18–1:20 (inferred) | A 1:2.29 forereef, a 10 m shelf, then the reef rise |
| Tube | Very hollow: about twice as long as wide | Rounder than any surveyed break; the Reef session is capping it at width/length 0.70 |
| Ride | 50–150 m, two to four barrel sections | Fast, often made only through the tube |
| The game's overturn fits | Near their upper edge: width/length 0.41–0.45, against about 0.5 measured | Far outside them, so the Reef's own rules apply |

Padang Padang is the easier target: its slope sits where the game's physics is sourced, while Teahupo'o sits beyond every dataset.

## What the game would need

1. **A Padang Padang bed.** It needs a reef edge angled to the swell as it wraps around the Bukit from the west-north-west, so the left peels fast but makeable, then a shallow coral shelf and a channel at the end. Its depths still need a source. Mead & Black surveyed neighbouring Bingin in 1999, and their bathymetric classification of world-class breaks may include Padang Padang. The Reef work's tools apply as they are: the peel predictor, the peel meter and the catch and ride reports.
2. **Its sea:** a long-period south-west groundswell, a south-east offshore wind, and low to mid tide as presets.
3. **The barrel and whitewater work from the Overview:** the swept overturn surface, a roller with body, and lip and spray shading. This is what makes it look like the footage. A new bed alone would still show the pocket and the thin, dark lip.

Steps 1–2 are about the size of the Reef's Part A. Step 3 is shared with every spot, and it is the one the footage depends on.
