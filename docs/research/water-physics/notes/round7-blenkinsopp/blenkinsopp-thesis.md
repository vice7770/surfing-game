# Blenkinsopp's thesis: the cavity under the jet against the reef's submergence (2026-10-03)

Blenkinsopp's 2007 PhD thesis at the University of Southampton, the work behind Blenkinsopp & Chaplin's papers, opened from Southampton's ePrints (record 466054, open access under its thesis licence) with the owner's OK. Read: the reef and flume (pp. 45–48), the breaking and vortex chapter (pp. 87–112), the void fractions (pp. 115–147) and scaling (pp. 202–204). Page numbers are the thesis's own.

- **Tags:** [measured] = the thesis's data; [fit] = our line through its figure 4.9, digitised from the scan (55 points); [inferred] = our reasoning.

## 1. The experiment

- A flume 0.7 m deep with a plastic reef 618 mm high: a 1:10 face and a zero-width crest that drops vertically, so waves plunge "straight into deep water" (pp. 47–48). Regular wave trains; the crest's depth h_c set to 62–102 mm by the water level (pp. 88, 95).
- The cavity ("vortex") was measured from side video at the plunge point, where the jet first strikes the water ahead, over 5 waves: length ±10 %, area ±20 % (pp. 99, 102). Side-wall friction let water into it (p. 99).
- Its area comes from the measured length and width through Longuet-Higgins's loop, A_v = (2√3/5) l_v w_v (eq. 4.9, p. 102), the game's own `LH82_AREA`: like with like.
- Seven plunging cases, H0/L0 0.0055–0.0331, at nine depths (p. 99). [inferred] With its waves' H0 of 40–105 mm (p. 91) over 0.7 m, ψ0 ≈ 0.16–0.20: beyond Pick & Feddersen's fits (0.089), where the Reef also lies.

## 2. What it measured

- **Length over width** [measured]: 1.46–2.28, mean 1.76, varying "in a non-systematic manner" with h_c/H_b and steepness (fig. 4.8, pp. 100–101; table 4.1 lists 2.29). Mead & Black's fit gives 1.47 for 1:10.
- **Area** [measured]: A_v/H_b² rises as the crest's relative submergence h_c/H_b falls, with no clear dependence on steepness (fig. 4.9, pp. 102–103, 111). [fit] Over h_c/H_b 0.67–1.28 it spans 0.04–0.41. Medians: 0.24 (h_c/H_b 0.67–0.76), 0.23 (to 0.87), 0.21 (to 0.98), 0.16 (to 1.10), 0.10 (to 1.28).
  - [fit] All points: A_v/H_b² ≈ 0.52 − 0.35 h_c/H_b (rms 0.05). The five long-period cases (H0/L0 ≤ 0.011; the Reef's 15–17 s swell is 0.004–0.007): **0.58 − 0.41 h_c/H_b** (rms 0.04).
  - [measured] At h_c 82 mm (table 5.1, p. 117): 0.331 at 0.5 Hz (H_b 95 mm, strongly plunging), 0.277 at 0.7 Hz (100 mm), 0.188 at 1.0 Hz (95 mm, spilling/plunging).
  - Pick & Feddersen quote the 2008 paper as 0.05–0.35 over H0/h_c 0.625–1.25.
- **Scale:** the cavity is taken to scale geometrically once H_b exceeds about 50 mm (after Couriel et al. 1998, pp. 202–203).
- **Not in the thesis:** the cavity's tilt, the jet's thickness, the jet's speed. Nearest: the plume and splash centroids move at about the linear shallow-water speed over the crest while entraining (p. 134).
- **Also** [measured]: the cavity's air is 62–74 % of all the air entrained, which peaks at 1.3–1.6 cavity volumes (pp. 130–131); the plume's peak mean void fraction is 17.5 % plunging, 13 % spilling (p. 147); entraining air takes at least 4–9 % of the dissipation, splash 2.5–5 % (p. 145); splash reaches about 1.2 H_b, droplets 5 H_b (pp. 141–142); the breaker depth index is 0.85 (p. 91).

## 4. Against the game's Reef

- **Void, 0.43 H²:**
  - The right size, by extrapolation, where the Reef's crest is shallowest for its waves: the line reaches 0.43 at h_c/H ≈ 0.37 (Big at mid tide; Medium and Big at low tide). The largest value measured is 0.41.
  - It agrees with our periodic runs: at their h_c/H of 0.43–0.52 the line gives 0.37–0.40, against their 0.34–0.45 at 85 % of the flight. The solitary runs' 0.085–0.089 are about a quarter of it.
  - Contradicted as a constant: at high tide, and for Practice and Small at mid tide (h_c/H 0.66–1.12), the line gives 0.12–0.31; the 44 points measured there run 0.06–0.41, median 0.22, none at 0.43. [inferred] Its 1:10 face is gentler than the Reef's 1:4.2 crossing, and steeper beds enlarge the void (Pick & Feddersen; O'Dea et al. 2021), so read these as floors.
- **Length over width, 1.42:** supported. The range 1.46–2.28 was measured in calm air; it holds the game's calm Reef (1.58 after the wind term) and starts 3 % above the offshore 1.42. The ratio didn't move with submergence, as the game holds it.
- **The 23° tilt and the 0.5 H lip:** no data here (our runs: 19°, 0.41–0.46 H).
- [inferred] At the plunge point the lab cavity is still round (1.46–2.28), as were the solitary runs at touchdown (1.74–2.12). Our periodic runs flatten to 3.3–4.0 just before landing, the lip falling into the drained flat, which the lab reef lacks.

## 5. Recommendation (to the owner)

1. Keep 0.43 H², 1.42, 23° and 0.5 H for now: published data checks our profiles rather than replacing them (the tube-build decision).
2. Let the Reef's periodic library span the submergence. Add a high-tide case (h_c 2.1 m) and the owed small case, and check each void against 0.58 − 0.41 h_c/H. If they follow it, the Reef's tube shrinks at high tide and in small swell; the thesis cites surfers on reefs going "hollower" on an ebb tide (Sayce et al. 1999).
3. Your option before those runs: the unswept Reef takes A_O/H² = min(0.43, 0.58 − 0.41 h_c/H), provisional. A 2.1 m Practice wave at high tide would draw its tube about 1.3 m long and 0.8 m tall instead of 2.1 by 1.3 m: too low for a crouched rider. Low-tide and Big tubes stay as they are.
