# Tube report · physical surf zone

Generated by `npm run report:tubes -- --seeds 2 --periods 12` on 2026-09-26 (plan P7; reported, not asserted).

**Conditions.** The Wave Lab defaults: Hs 1.4 m, Tp 10 s, spreading s 12. Stage 2 (Boussinesq) on the CPU. Seeds 1–2, 12 peak periods each.

**How tubes are measured.** A break is a plunging jet where the local Iribarren number under its crest is 0.4–2, and a spilling roller below 0.4. A jet leaves at the crest's own speed, measured from the surface's motion on its front face (JET_SPEED_RATIO 1, provisional), over 0.25 s (provisional). Each landed lip parcel is measured in the frame of the crest it left: its tube length is how far ahead of the advancing crest it landed, its height the drop to the surface it landed on. A lip landing less than 0.1 m ahead of the crest, or overrun by it, draws no tube. Step times are wall-clock on a shared machine. The measured range is width-to-length 0.25–1 (Feddersen et al. 2023 at Surf Ranch; passyworld up to round).
