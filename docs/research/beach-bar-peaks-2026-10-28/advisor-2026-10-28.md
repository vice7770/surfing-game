## A correction to the earlier advice

All geometry below uses the Canyon's built 40° arms (tan θ 0.84) and a 12 m tip rounding, not the 60° calibrated from Snell. Down each arm the swell falls to 0.65–0.75 of the tip's.

## 1. The background profile

**The arms are too short on today's Dean profile.**
- With a planar 1:47 V, Medium's tip break (2.5–3.0 m) is 15–22 m seaward of the background's own break contour (Dean: 2.5 m at z −95, 3.0 m at −125).
- The arms meet that contour after about 27–37 m and close out.
- Relief at the tip is only 0.3–0.5 m, and Big barely sees the bar.

**Build:**
- Background = min(5 m, Dean + Δ), with Δ = 2 m seaward of about z −50, eased to 0 at the waterline.
- That gives a face of about 1:15–1:20 in the rips, effectively the Canyon's floor. Medium's background break contour then sits about 35–45 m from the waterline, and about 2 m of relief stands at the tip's break contour (inferred from the Canyon).
- Apply it across the whole window, so the bar is the only thing that varies along shore.
