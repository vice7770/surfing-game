# Hold-down report

The wipeout spec's Part B checks hold-downs against the gameplay survey's §6: in 1–2 m plunging surf a surfer's hold-down mostly lasts 5–15 s (surf media and coaching; no measured study), set by the aerated, turbulent water under the roller, not by a timer.

Riders lie 8 m inside the canyon break point on the practice swell (Hs 1.4 m, Tp 12 s), 5 along the shore for each way of taking it. Each is knocked off its board as broken water (breaking ≥ 0.3) reaches it and watched for 30 s: relaxed, or swimming up (stroking) from 2 s. A **hold-down** is the longest single stretch with the head under water. The riders are ghosts: they feel the water, its air and turbulence, and the lip, and push back on none of it.

2 seed(s), 3 simulated min each. Run time 615 s for 360 s of sea.
