## Limits

This is an approximate CPU/F64 mirror of cubic sampling and planar-chord geometry. The real GLSL uses F32 and its vector/dot arithmetic; no bit-exact raster claim follows. It measures one heightfield's geometric interpolation risk only. It does not prove depth/MSAA coverage, foam/slope interpolation, caustics, mask seams, carved tube geometry, cavity air, mouth/touchdown behavior, silhouette quality or FPS. Native matched screenshots and actual renderer measurements remain the candidate's authority. Halving patch spacing density is a graphics approximation, not an exact optimization or physics change.
