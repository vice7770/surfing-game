# Exact clock and cap-query cost repair

This archive preserves an **unadopted cost repair** to the bounded C candidate. It removes full contour construction from clock-only queries and from the two cap-velocity derivative stencils. Geometry coefficients, precision rules, current/prospective water and air semantics, clocks, F32 positions and cap transport remain identical on the recorded queries. The root built this isolated source successfully. No native FPS improvement, rolling appearance, sealing/retirement view, body clearance or ordinary entry/pass is established by this repair.

`ProfileLibrary.profileTimes` previously called the full query at the authored event. Every uncached query also built a current contour and two full finite-difference contours, each including the inner offset and bounded root/floor. The optional recursive velocity flag in `sampleBoundedC` was disabled by the library; the redundant stencils came from `ProfileLibrary.analyticAt` itself. Drawing, sheet and analytic crash queries usually did not consume boundary velocity. Library FIFO caches remain 64 entries and the provider event cache remains 128; actual cache-miss rates were not measured.

The repair adds an exact lifecycle-only query from the same final carrier and parameter blend. The boundary derivative still uses its original moving-carrier ages, dt and F32 metric stage order, but reads a roof/cap-only evaluator at those ages. One full current contour remains authoritative and validated. Public `profileAt` still returns boundary velocity, and analytic contact uses it; water flow remains its distinct metadata channel. The full numerical sampler body and all its existing shape helpers are byte-identical. No approximation, time quantization, broad cache rewrite, raw fallback or stale per-case derived table was introduced. The lightweight cap evaluator intentionally does not construct or validate an unused inner root/floor at its stencil age; the recorded cap-stencil comparison covers that change in work.
