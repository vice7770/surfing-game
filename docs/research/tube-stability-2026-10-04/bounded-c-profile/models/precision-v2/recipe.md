## Unchanged spatial budget with the new envelope operation

The budget remains `.001*max(W,H)`. V1's `h*size` proxy describes a whole-roof snap that v2 does not perform, so it is not the displacement of the new operation. Instead compare the unresolved T=0 outer cubic control points against a same-phase representable-thickness continuation with `Tref=max(requested,8ULP)`. The latter uses the identical roof construction at the SAME `g,h` and cap datum. Also compare the unresolved tail controls to the SAME-phase reference resolved tail controls: `F1=(qC+Tref,yC+slope*Tref)`, plane tangent unchanged, and the same blended/capped handle law. The maximum Euclidean control difference bounds the respective Bezier curve displacement by convexity. A precision collapse exceeding the unchanged budget is still a domain failure. No relaxed tolerance or raw fallback.

This control bound covers retained roof/tail envelope movement. It does not claim per-index continuity of all collapsed cap/inner/root samples: as a nearly coincident loop becomes unresolvable, formerly separated indices can coalesce along a retained boundary. Report per-index switch displacement separately, plus sampled nearest-segment distances for the actual discrete contour, residual air and crossings.
