## Result

No concrete camera integration blocker was found in the current frozen files. The expanded sampling resolves the previous three-cell blind spot and the previous direction/component errors. The policy records the selected geometry and can fail honestly without changing the eye or seeking another state.

The interior diagnostics have two explicit failure strings: `no-strict-air-column-in-declared-whole-directed-interval` and `full-selected-eye-to-candidate-segment-obstructed`. Below-surface and exterior-obstruction failures are retained as named booleans (`eyeBelowSurface`, `targetBelowSurface`, `exterior.belowSurface`, `exterior.fullSightline.indexedLoftOccluded`, and the corresponding exterior checkpoint fields), rather than an additional combined exterior failure string. `exterior.failureRetained` refers to the inherited **interior** failure only. A null interior failure therefore does not mean the exterior pose is above water or unobstructed. Native visual review must read those exterior diagnostics independently. This is a reporting interpretation, not a requirement to hunt for a clearer pose or change the frozen launch.

## Limits needed for interpreting this run

The target is the closest sampled strict-air station to the directed unformed/run boundary among 24 deterministic stations. It need not be exactly the boundary, and a narrow opening between samples can be missed. A null target is therefore a policy failure, not proof that no entrance exists. A clear indexed center segment and strict endpoint column are point-camera geometry observations; they do not prove a finite eye/body clearance volume, a rendered visible mouth, or an exterior-to-interior connected air corridor. No existing local canopy screenshot becomes accepted because the camera policy is better.

The actual current eye component gate is localId equality in the same sampler epoch. It correctly excludes another disconnected component with the same front ID. It does not replace the separate positive exported point-ID lineage gates. Those lineage gates remain inherited and are not re-audited here.
