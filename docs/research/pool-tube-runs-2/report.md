- **Per H at vertical:** H is crest minus the trough ahead at the frame the face goes vertical (`plunge_measure.py`), the height the game sizes its jets with; the jet and tube are just before touchdown (the last overturned frame with the jet at least 3 cells off the face).
- **Fits:** Pick & Feddersen's, at ψ0 = S1/A0^¼ with S1 = the face's slope along the ray (the solver's measured Mead & Black X), as `scripts/barrel-library.ts` takes them. They are plane-slope fits; Padang Padang's reef cases ran under them in area.
- **Cells across the lip:** basilisk-profiles.md's rule asks for about 6. Under 4, rerun phase 2 one level finer (`LEVEL2=14 tools/basilisk/run_pool.sh`; phase 1 is reused).
- **A0** is the second crest's height as it passes the platform (h0) (phase 1, interpolated), the key the swept barrel blends cases by; `train` means the foot crest wasn't tracked and the cnoidal train's crest stands in.

## Files

- `data/`: per case, `periodic_<case>_L13_library.json` (the 128-point profiles, `periodic_library.py`), `_metrics.json` and `_plunge.json` (the times and the tube, `plunge_measure.py`), `_track.json`, `_p1_track.json` and `_scout_track.json` (the second crest, `crest_track.py`), `_params.txt` and `_train.json`.
- `barrel-index.json` and `barrel-cases.md`: the cases' index entries and validation table, from `npm run barrels` (written for `pool_ledge_big`, `pool_ledge_small`, `pool_kneeface_big`, `pool_kneeface_small`, `pool_knee_big`, `pool_knee_small`, `pool_steep9_big`, `pool_steep9_small`); their files are in `docs/research/pool-tube-runs-2/barrels`.
- `summary.json`: every number above. Figures (`*_strip.png`, `*_tube.png`, `*_overview.png`) when matplotlib was there.

## For the swept barrel's wiring

- Candidates, not the game's cases: their `.bin` files are in `barrels/` here, not in `public/barrels`, and no game index lists them.
- **The decision rule** (the advisor's step-7 ruling, §2.2): the ledge is adopted if its usable clearance (the clearance held over a 0.3 m depth along the ray, a crouched body, at life 0.5–0.9, at game scale) is at least 1.3× the knee case's at the same face height, and its lip lands within 0.6–1.6 H. Then the floor change (`POOL_STRAIGHT`), its probes, and these cases into the game.
