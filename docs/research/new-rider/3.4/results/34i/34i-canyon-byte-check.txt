**A kept `.sea` does not reproduce a fresh spin-up's hashes, confirmed on `main` with nothing else changed.** The
caution is real, it is not about this branch, and it is why every pairing above is like for like.

##### why 120 steps and not 3,600

The machine ran out of memory: 8 GB of RAM with **57 MB free** (`vm_stat`: 3,577 free pages of 16 KB) and swapping
onto a volume with **5.5 GB free at 98 % full**. A 3,600-step arm that takes about 10 minutes rested took over an
hour and was abandoned twice. The stepping rate itself is unaffected when run in the foreground (77.7–85.8 ms a step
of CPU solver, in line with step 3.4h's 84.3–88.4), so the cost is wall clock and not the measurement.

**What 120 steps does and does not buy.** It hashes 120 consecutive snapshots and the whole final state, over two
seconds of simulated sea, which is enough for any difference in the board's or the rider's code to appear — the
rider is stepped on every one of them (`board` 0.98–1.08 ms p50 in both columns). It is NOT the folder's own 3,600
and it must not be quoted against the folder's own reference hashes.
