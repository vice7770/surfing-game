# Step 3.4n. The side force decomposed on BOTH sides, term by term, on the same ticks.
#
# THE QUESTION (the one the track narrowed to): why is the model's side force wrong-signed in crouch and compress,
# before the fall, below 4 m/s? 3.4m proved no row of `PLANT_SPEEDS` can produce the observed sign, so the fault had
# to be in HOW `Y` is assembled, in a STATE fed to it, or in a term the model has no derivative for.
#
# THE ANSWER: a STATE. The probe derives the slip as `beta = atan2(v_left, v_fwd)` -- + sliding toward the board's
# LEFT -- and feeds it to derivatives measured against the lab's slip, `atan2(-v_left, v_fwd)`, + sliding RIGHT
# [R, `../roll-plant/plant.ts:368`; `rollModel.ts`'s `boardDerivatives` flips the lab's N and Y into the model's axes
# and does NOT flip the slip variable]. `sideV` is the board's local +x, and +x is its LEFT
# (`finForces.ts`: `side: 1` is "the board's left"; `BoardBody.buildFoils` puts it at `+x`). So the slip term of
# `yModel` has carried the wrong sign since step 3.3p, and it is the model's LARGEST term exactly where beta is
# large, which is below 4 m/s. Second, smaller cause: 45-56 % of the game's own side force in the owner's cell is
# added mass + wave radiation, which the twelve derivatives DO NOT CONTAIN (measured: at +1 deg of slip off straight
# the lab's dY/dbeta by part is fins 315.1, rails 22.9, friction 11.5, pressure -11.5, buoyancy 0.0,
# waterInertia 0.0, radiation 0.0 at 4 m/s [R, `../../3.2/results/plant-speeds.jsonl`]), and in that cell the
# inertial part OPPOSES the quasi-static one on 0.79-1.00 of ticks.
#
# THE RIG. `3.3/plant.ts` with: the game's ten water side forces each dumped on its own (they sum to `yGame`
# identically); the model's four side-force addends, their four derivatives and their four states; the
# counterfactual `yModelNegB` (the slip term's sign flipped and nothing else); the controller's own slip estimate;
# and `SLIP=lab`, a one-line opt-in that puts the derived state in the lab's frame. No byte under `src/` or
# `server/` is changed by this round.
#
