======================================================================================================================
4b. THE CLOSURE: is the water's whole lateral answer to a bank just the DECK NORMAL, projected?
======================================================================================================================
   The contact's reaction on the board is minus the rider's contact force. Its world-horizontal across part
   is -cFy*sin(roll) (cFy is the normal, N, on the board's own up), so a water force that merely balances it
   must read fwH = +cFy*sin(roll) + m_board*a_H, and m_board is 2.54 kg. If that closes, the water makes
   essentially NO force in the plane of the deck across it — which is the component `j.x` (and so `dLever`,
   and so `across`) is built from, and it is what the 2026-11-15 consult-log row predicted for a flat hull.
