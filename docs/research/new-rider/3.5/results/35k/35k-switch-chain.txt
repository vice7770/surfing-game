################ Step 3.5k — the switch chain, hop by hop, from a search string to a rider's own field

                              search        switch    SurfZoneConfig        runner hop        live rider
                              (none)             —                 —                 —         unbounded
                     ?twistGrip=feet          feet              feet              feet           BOUNDED
                     ?twistGrip=FEET          feet              feet              feet           BOUNDED
                   ?twistGrip= feet           feet              feet              feet           BOUNDED
                       ?twistGrip=on             —                 —                 —         unbounded
                      ?twistGrip=yes             —                 —                 —         unbounded
                        ?twistGrip=1             —                 —                 —         unbounded
                      ?twistGrip=off             —                 —                 —         unbounded
                     ?twistGrip=true             —                 —                 —         unbounded

  ⚠️ `FEET` and ` feet ` are lower-cased and trimmed by the switch; `on`, `yes`, `1`, `true` and `off` are
  NOT values and must all read `—`. Only `feet` reaches the config. The `runner hop` column is
  `SurfZoneRunner`'s own forwarding expression written out in this probe. ⭐ `live rider` does NOT use that
  copy: it constructs a REAL `SurfZoneRunner` from the config and reads `runner.session.rider.`
  `twistStopBounded` off the `AttachedRider` the runner built for itself — so the hop a `swing` option was
  once dropped in is exercised for real, and the column is the host's own object, not the URL passed in.
