## 4. The planing gate, which is the other half of what the switch changes

Shipped, the cap is `planing ? held : 0`, and the latch leaves the plane below 3 m/s and re-enters it at 4 [R]. So off the plane the shipped ask cap is **exactly 0** — the rider asks to stand plumb however hard the stick is held — where the formula would allow:

| v, m/s | shipped cap (off the plane) | formula at 4.5 m | formula at 2 m |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.50 | 0.00 | 0.32 | 0.73 |
| 1.00 | 0.00 | 1.30 | 2.92 |
| 1.30 | 0.00 | 2.19 | 4.92 |
| 2.00 | 0.00 | 5.18 | 11.52 |
| 2.50 | 0.00 | 8.06 | 17.67 |
| 2.99 | 0.00 | 11.45 | 24.50 |

So `?askCap=4.5` keeps the shipped radius and removes only the gate: above the plane it is the
shipped cap to the bit, and below it the formula at 4.5 m. That is why it is the arm that isolates the gate.
