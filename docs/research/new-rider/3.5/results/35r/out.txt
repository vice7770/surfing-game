### 2.4 The in-plane inverse: `chain = cos(flexion ÷ 2)`

For equal thigh and shin the 3D chain is exactly `cos(flexion ÷ 2)`, so **flexion = 2·arccos(chain)**. Checked against the probe's own 3D readings over 145 poses (5 subjects × 29 depths, thigh/shin 0.89–1.03): deviation **0.00–0.14 points**, upper-middle 0.01. So a reader with an admissible frame can invert the chain to a knee angle; §2.1–2.3 say what to do when the frame is not admissible, which is **do not**.
