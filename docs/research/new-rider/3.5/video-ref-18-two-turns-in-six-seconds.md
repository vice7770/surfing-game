### 2.4 — ⚠️ A composition bug in my own sheet code, found and worked around, stated so no one repeats it

`drawImage(v, sx, sy, sw, sh, x, y, tw, th-12)` scales **x and y by different factors whenever the tile's aspect differs from the crop's**. Several of my survey sheets were therefore **anisotropic** (e.g. 0.788 across and 0.576 down).

**It does not corrupt anything published here**, because sheets were used only to (a) find moments and (b) compare **vertical order** — is the hand above the crown — which any monotone y-map preserves. **Every length, ratio and angle in this document comes from a single gridded frame with a source-coordinate grid drawn into it at a stated magnification.** But the warning belongs in the skill beside "survey strips lie about angles": **a survey strip can also lie about ASPECT, silently, because of the tile geometry rather than the source.**
