# Canyon surfer movement review

Reviewed `main` at `9522a9eb` on 18 November 2026. Vicente plays Canyon and judges the feel. The reference move he chose is a bottom turn that carries speed into the climb. This is advice for the surfer-physics builder. An automated rider and gameplay scoring are deferred.

Claude's movement coordinator recorded the advisory scope in commit `41fe8c9c`, independently checked the hip-stop impulse and visual pelvis compensation in source, and acknowledged both findings. [Coordination record](../../../superpowers/handoffs/2026-11-16-surfer-and-wave-handoff.md#L1798)

## What to address first

**1. Make a fold at the hips mean the same thing in the body and on screen.** The new rider already integrates a hip angle and applies a reaction to the body and board. The standing posture rebuilds the pelvis, torso and head from fixed points and translates them together. Their relative geometry stays fixed as the rotor turns. The physical centre of mass and the inertia computed from those points therefore miss the fold. The visual rig calculates a separate target from leg depth and the current pose, then moves the drawn pelvis back to preserve its estimated mass position along the fold. That is how a crouch can look animated while the balance model still carries an upright torso. [Hip drive](../../../../src/physics/standingBody.ts#L272) · [Posture](../../../../src/physics/AttachedRider.ts#L5499) · [Mass and inertia](../../../../src/physics/AttachedRider.ts#L5837) · [Visual fold](../../../../src/scene/rig/HumanoidRig.ts#L1385) · [Visual pelvis compensation](../../../../src/scene/rig/HumanoidRig.ts#L1937)

For the builder, define the hip pivot, axis and sign in the rider's frame first. The rotor reacts in board pitch, whereas the drawn surfer folds toward their toes and can turn their chest relative to the board. Project the physical upper-body direction into the rig's frame for regular and goofy stance. Let physical torso and head positions feed the mass calculation and the visual target, preserving planted feet. Carry their relative velocity through posture updates and detachment. Keep the existing torque reaction accounted for once. The current hip drive responds to excess ankle demand, so connecting it resolves inconsistent geometry but leaves deliberate folding during trim as a separate design question.

**2. Account for the impulse at the hip joint's end stop.** With sustained outward demand, the braking-distance check cuts the outward drive. At sufficient demand the return term is also zero, which lets the rotor coast to the joint limit. The code then sets its outward speed to zero. The reaction calculation receives only the previously computed torque. A stop from angular speed ω to zero removes angular momentum of magnitude 8 × |ω| N·m·s from the rotor, with no corresponding stop impulse passed to the other body. A narrowing stance range can also engage this limit. Model the stop reaction, or brake within range using a paired torque. This is a code defect under that condition. Its frequency and effect in Vicente's rides are unknown. [Stop](../../../../src/physics/standingBody.ts#L299) · [Reaction](../../../../src/physics/standingBody.ts#L377)

**3. Distinguish a turn cue from a balance recovery.** The balance code derives its arm and knee responses from the same hip/capture signal. The arm value affects inertia and carries no independent left/right hand trajectory. The rig already has stage- and steering-driven movement. Keep those paths distinct when interpreting footage. Clip 10 justifies allowing an arm to move before a bottom turn and remain quiet through the following redirect. A universal arm lead, or faster knee timing on every turn, would bake one rider's choice into the controls. [Arm and knee trigger](../../../../src/physics/humanBalance.ts#L423) · [Body-channel survey](body-channels.md)

**4. Check the speed frame when steering feels dead.** The default steering bank request is zero off plane. The latch enters above 4 m/s and leaves below 3 m/s, measured horizontally relative to local water during riding. Within that band, the previous state matters. Separately, an already-standing test placement seeds the latch from the magnitude of world velocity, including current and vertical motion. That makes placement evidence ambiguous in the transition band. Ordinary paddle-in starts prone and reaches standing through a different transition. The placement discrepancy alone cannot explain a problem after a normal pop-up. [Steering](../../../../src/physics/AttachedRider.ts#L3531) · [Mount](../../../../src/physics/AttachedRider.ts#L2857) · [Planing](../../../../src/physics/AttachedRider.ts#L3495) · [Pop-up](../../../../src/physics/AttachedRider.ts#L2738)

## Map from surfing to the game

| Moment | What the player and wave contribute | What the builder should preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paddle, catch, pop up | Paddling puts the board into the moving face. The player chooses when to get up. | Carry the actual board speed, orientation and contact through the transfer from hands to feet. |
| Trim down the line | The rider picks a line on the face and shifts pressure fore or aft. | A quiet ride is valid surfing. The torso can fold with little knee change and both feet can stay planted. |
| Bottom turn | The rider arrives low with speed, loads a rail, and redirects the board across the face. | The travelled path curves. Distinguish bank against the local face, board heading, and the direction the board actually travels. A board can yaw or slide with little change in path. |
| Release into the climb | The rider releases the loaded rail and projects up the open face with speed left to use. | Relate the climb to the moving crest. Water lifting a board vertically can look like a climb. Preserve the timing differences among arms, legs, torso and board. |
| Top turn or cutback | The lip or shoulder presents a different place to redirect. The rider can stay folded as the board comes around. | Allow a different body sequence from the bottom turn. Avoid prescribing one crouch-and-release cycle for every direction change. |
| Pump, tube or exit | The rider may cycle pressure, stay low under the lip, or settle into a long trim. A lost rail or exhausted contact can lead to a fall. | Allow both short compressions and held lows. Treat horizontal speed, board support and available headroom as distinct. |

The study already cited in this project recorded **835 turns in total**, across six surfers and 227 waves. Its board measurements give context for turn duration, angle and speed. The project's 3.8 m radius and 1.41 g estimates are derived from speed and angular rate. They are not directly measured paths or acceptance thresholds for Vicente's Canyon turn. The paper's turn-flow ratio compares successive manoeuvres, while exit/entry speed within a single bottom turn is a different quantity. [Forsyth et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-58387-y) · [Project derivation](../../surf-gameplay-research.md)

A correction to the clip 10 update in `2dc6bdf3` matters for future tuning. Its **0.26 s** covers first visible tail spray to the end of the board's swing. Its **0.87 s** covers first arm movement to the top of the climb. The published mean quoted in the update is **0.96 ± 0.04 s**. The arithmetic band is 0.92–1.00 s, so 0.87 s lies outside it. The band around a group mean is also a different quantity from the range of individual turns. Keep both video timestamps and use them for sequencing. Neither figure establishes a required turn duration. [Window definitions](video-ref-10-mentawai-drone.md#L117)

**Scope of verification.** I read the implementation and research notes, inspected saved video frames from reads 7–10, and exchanged messages with the Claude movement coordinator. Before the owner clarified the advisory role, an independent reviewer ran four focused unit suites (42 passes), and I sampled six synthetic standing starts on one smooth face. Under held Compress, the three stick positions produced identical recorded paths at the 2 m/s entry and different paths at the 5 m/s entry. All six had zero hip-rotor angle, so those observations cannot demonstrate the end-stop defect or how a moving trunk feels. Vicente's own rides supply that evidence.
