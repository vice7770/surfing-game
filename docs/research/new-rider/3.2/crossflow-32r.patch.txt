Step 3.2r: Shuford's cross-flow term for a planing face, built, measured and PARKED.

Apply with:  git apply docs/research/new-rider/3.2/crossflow-32r.patch.txt

WHAT IT IS. `hullForces.patchForce` gives a wetted hull face Savitsky's small-trim planing coefficient
(PRESSURE_COEFFICIENT = 0.4575, his fit's own validity being 2-15 degrees of trim) at every incidence, and adds the
cross-flow term only when the board is a FULLY SUBMERGED plate (`deep`, the duck-dive branch). Against NACA TN 3939's
Table I at lambda = 3 that is 30-46 % low at 12-30 degrees of incidence. This patch gives a planing face Shuford's
eq. (23) instead: C_N = CROSS_FLOW_LINEAR * sin(a) + (4/3) * sin^2(a) * cos(a), with CROSS_FLOW_LINEAR derived (not
tuned) as whatever is left of this file's existing anchor once the cross-flow term is taken out of it at the same
tau = 4 degrees, lambda = 3, so ordinary trim is unchanged to the last digit. Both coefficients ramp back to
`SUBMERGED`'s own law by `submergedShare`, so the duck-dive force is byte-identical. The cos factor is held at its
35-degree value past 35 degrees (eq. 23's outer cos tau sends C_N to zero at 90, which is untested and unphysical).

VERIFIED. C_L on the wetted area against TN 3939's measurements at lambda = 3: 12 deg -30 % -> -3 %, 18 deg -37 % ->
+4 %, 24 deg -44 % -> +5 %, 30 deg -46 % -> +11 %. Exactly neutral at 4 degrees by construction; +20 % at 8.

WHY IT IS PARKED, measured on the owner's acceptance test (24 runs, `n-old-cross-32r.jsonl`, `n-new-cross-32r.jsonl`
against `n-old-32r.jsonl`, `n-new-32r.jsonl`):

  old rider: gate 12 of 12 -> 11 of 12; n median 1.085 -> 1.083; path rate 33.99 -> 32.04 deg/s; kept at 1 s
             1.052 -> 1.046; kept at 2 s 0.818 -> 0.837
  new rider: gate 1 of 12 -> 1 of 12; n median 1.047 -> 1.056; path rate 32.63 -> 32.03 deg/s; kept at 1 s
             1.023 -> 1.018

It changes the carve by nothing, because OUR MEDIAN INCIDENCE IS 6 DEGREES (5.2-8.1 old, 3.2-9.3 new) and the law is
neutral at 4 and +20 % at 8. The correction lives at 12-30 degrees, which our board only reaches once the face is
steep enough to give a real bottom turn. And it is not free: run alone, these ten default-path assertions fail in
`src/physics/AttachedRider.test.ts` -

  rider coupled to the board > lets go once it tips beyond recovery, and stops loading the board
  lean, trim, crouch and heading hold > tucks deeper than Compress under a tube's curl and stands back up
  the banked body (turn redesign) > holds a crouched full-steer turn on flat water at 8 m/s
  the banked body (turn redesign) > holds Compress taken mid-turn on flat water at 7 m/s
  the banked body (turn redesign) > holds Compress taken mid-turn on flat water at 11 m/s
  the banked body (turn redesign) > holds Compress taken mid-turn at 11 m/s no worse than before the top-turn plan
  the banked body (turn redesign) > makes a deep U at the bottom of the face      (expected > 30, got 9.04)
  the banked body (turn redesign) > keeps standing and turns through ninety degrees after a deep manual tuck
  the banked body (turn redesign) > changes rail from a carve at 6 m/s
  the balance margin > falls below 0.3 ... and recovers                           (expected < 0.3, got 0.3007)

all ten real AssertionErrors, no timeouts. The deep-U one is a large regression, and the advisor's own warning is the
cause: the cross-flow force is along the patch normal, so at a big incidence a large part of it is backwards along
the path - it is drag in proportion to sin(a) as well as lift.

WHEN TO TAKE IT OFF THE SHELF. When the wave track gives a face of 30-45 degrees over about 2 m (Part 16 section 3),
the hull will run at the incidences where this law was measured and the present one is 30-46 % low. Then it is worth
the default path, the ten tests above are the old law's behaviour and should be re-baselined with the owner, and the
pressure-centre law (ours 0.667 fixed, measured 0.69-0.80 varying) comes right as a consequence rather than a tune.
