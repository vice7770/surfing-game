=== A NOTE ON frames-world-off.jsonl ===
The probe's TRACE writes every column of the Frame record; the committed file is TRIMMED to the columns the two tables
above are computed from -- t, roll, rollRate, hullShare, dryShare, planeShare, bnS, bbS, bhS, fS, fN -- at 6
significant figures, because the full record is 42 MB for these 13,422 frames and this repository is not the place for
it. Every number in Part 28 2 and 4 was re-derived from the trimmed file after trimming and is unchanged. To rebuild
the full record:

  for each of the nine cells' world-off legs (STEER=0 | STEER=+-0.25/0.5/1 STEER_SIGN=roll STEER_FROM=0.3 |
    VERTICAL=particle | HULL=rail), with RIDER=new STANCES=compress,tall EXTRAS=-3,-2,-1,0,1.5,3 and
    TRACE=<one file> pointed at the SAME file (TRACE appends).
