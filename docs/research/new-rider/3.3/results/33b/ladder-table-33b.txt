Step 3.3b, the gain deficit split by source. One row a cell, twelve runs a cell, the whole pool of frames.
Every number is a POOLED least-squares slope over every frame of the twelve runs in that cell: d(the deck-frame
tilt)/d(the deck's roll), in degrees of tilt per degree of roll, so they add. 'measured' is the test's own
tiltAcross on the roll; 'force' is the instantaneous in-plane water force's share; 'lag' is the rest, which is
the 0.1 s low pass of the felt vertical against a rolling deck and is not a force at all.
