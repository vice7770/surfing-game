## Limits of this review

The guards match CPU source/oracle files by their exact byte/hash words and unique relative-path suffix, rather than independently requiring the preparation/oracle root paths. CPU `checkInputs` are pinned without a separate helper assertion naming every expected fixture/config file. Provenance therefore also relies on the root-authored, pinned CPU receipt and pinned preparation readiness. This is a bounded guard limitation, not evidence of a mismatched actual run; I did not inspect that receipt.

Lexical identifier presence does not prove execution of every field assignment. Finite native output verifies publication only on the actual samples captured. The unchanged marker must qualify the captured aggregate values, and the water impulse aggregates still combine radiation and entrainment. These helper checks do not separate individual hydrodynamic mechanisms or establish a causal fix, visual quality, FPS, tube/body passage, production adoption, or general physics correctness.

No executable result is claimed by this reviewer. Root must produce and accept the new actual build/diagnostic/helper receipts and seal before any native arm. Source review alone does not authorize or perform a run.

No unresolved concrete source defect was found in the final reviewed helper sources. This is limited source approval of the proposed guard and adapter design, with the provenance limitations above; it is not executable or native acceptance. Reviewed source/authority pins are recorded separately in `independent-helper-review-pins.json` without pinning mutable future acceptance metadata or root outputs.
