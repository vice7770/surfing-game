# Ride report

Generated by `npm run report:ride -- --ghosts --style turns --seeds 2 --minutes 5` (2026-09-26, 58.4 min). Natural sea (Wave Lab defaults), 2 seed(s) × 5 min per spot, stage 2 on the CPU.

The runner's own rider rides, pushing back on the water, with 6 ghost riders along the break (they feel the water and the lip and push back on neither). An autopilot waits 5 m outside the break line, paddles when a crest rises 0.35 m behind it, pops up on the cue and rides S-turns: a crouched bottom turn toward the peel low on the face (below 35 % of its height), a top turn sitting back high on it (above 70 %, a snap in a breaking crest), a cutback from more than 8 m out on the shoulder, crouched on the way down and extended climbing. Rides of 3 s or more are measured every step against the wave under them (the wave-frame gauge).

**Research ranges** (the [surf-science survey](surf-gameplay-research.md) §1): accomplished surfers average 6.4 m/s and top out at 9.7 m/s (Forsyth et al. 2024); competitors peak at 9–12.5 m/s (Farley et al. 2012); the required speed is c / sin α (Walker 1974; Hutt et al. 2001); trim sits on the lower-to-mid face (Sugimoto 1998).
