Generated by `npm run report:rideability -- --seeds 3 --periods 20` on 2026-09-26 (plan §4 item 11; reported, not asserted).

**Conditions.** The Wave Lab defaults: Hs 1.4 m, Tp 10 s, 10° from shore-normal, spreading s 12, tide 0 m, calm wind. Game resolution: 1 m surf-zone cells and a 160 m along-shore window. Each spot runs seeds 1–3 for 20 peak periods after the spin-up. The peel is sampled once per period from the onsets of that period.

**Reading it.** A wave is makeable by a skill level when its peel angle reaches that level's minimum (Hutt, Black & Mead 2001: professional 27°, advanced 29°, intermediate 40°, beginner 60°). The shares are cumulative: every beginner wave is also a professional wave. A close-out peels at under 27°. "Mixed" means the onset fit found several peaks at once, so no single angle applies. Shares are of the sampled periods that had a break.

**Known limits.**
- Ride-time distributions need a rider in the physical mode (P4), so they are not reported yet.
- The stage 2 solver is Madsen–Sørensen Boussinesq with Kennedy eddy-viscosity breaking (P5). Its dispersion keeps shoaling waves from breaking early, but where contours meet the swell nearly square (the Beach, Point and Reef) the break has little along-crest gradient to peel along and mostly closes out; the Canyon, whose bed bends the crests, peels. Peeling needs shaped spots.
- The peel is measured over the along-shore window only, with 10 % of the columns at each open edge left out.
