# Decision note: breaking-wave representation

**Current decision:** keep `InteractiveWaterField` as the bulk-water source for board support, flow, and break timing. The detached lip now has a separate bounded 3D parcel and collision model; its rendered mesh reads the same parcels that contact the rider. See [ADR 0003](../adr/0003-plunging-sheet-collision.md). This hybrid does not resolve full tube flow or replace the base field with a 3D fluid solver.

| Representation | What it can show | Gameplay authority | Cost and limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Current single-valued height field `y = h(x,z,t)` | Traveling face, crest steepening, a dissipating break, flattened/reforming **surface height**, and surface foam color | Existing field directly drives board contacts and local water flow | Fixed 97 × 161 CPU grid and matching dynamic mesh; one height per horizontal location cannot contain a folded lip, tunnel, falling sheet, trapped air, or vertical jet. [Thürey et al. (2007)](https://matthias-research.github.io/pages/publications/breakingWaves.pdf) |
| Height field plus bounded 3D lip/spray/foam | A local overhang and whitewater while retaining deterministic rides | The field drives the bulk wave; the connected lip parcels own local rider collision and the thickened lip mesh | Parcel-span capsule contact approximates the mesh; no resolved pressure, air, or full tube flow. Hybrid connected-particle sheets are a precedent for real-time breaking effects. [Thürey et al. (2007)](https://matthias-research.github.io/pages/publications/breakingWaves.pdf) |
| Full 3D free surface (for example SPH or a volume/level-set method) | Plunging jets, entrained air, overturning, and three-dimensional flow around the crest | Can become water/contact authority after replacing the board coupling and validation path | Larger state, more solver work, and a separate surface/foam rendering problem. A surf-zone SPH study resolved spilling and plunging breakers over beach and reef bathymetry and compared them with flume data; this is a different fidelity and development scope. [Lowe et al. (2020)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.00827) |

## Evidence relevant to this game

- The present field stores exactly one elevation and horizontal flow vector at each grid cell. That topology can steepen and dissipate a face, but it cannot fold water over itself. `PlungingSheet` now spawns ballistic parcels from the field's active break, `PlungingSheetMesh` renders connected thickened faces, and the board queries parcel contact. The hybrid method by Thürey et al. detects steep fronts in a shallow-water height field, spawns connected sheets, and turns their impact into spray/foam. Its authors explicitly frame the goal as the *visual effect* of breaking, not a full turbulence solution. [Thürey et al., *Real-time Breaking Waves for Shallow Water Simulations* (2007)](https://matthias-research.github.io/pages/publications/breakingWaves.pdf)
- Whitewater should persist for a short period after the active lip has passed and then decay. Laboratory and field measurements found comparable **patterns** of whitecap foam growth and decay after scaling, but their absolute sizes and durations differ. This supports a local, time-decaying foam history, not a universal lifetime constant. [Callaghan, Deane & Stokes, *A Comparison of Laboratory and Field Measurements of Whitecap Foam Evolution From Breaking Waves* (2024)](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2023JC020193)
- “Reforming” has two meanings here. A dissipated crest can regain a smoother single-valued outline within the existing field after the break gate moves on. A **physical second shoaling and break** would require incoming wave energy plus validated initiation and termination criteria; coastal approaches advect breaker state shoreward. The game now has a synthetic shelf but only one launched packet and an authored peel gate, so any second crest or renewed break remains authored behavior. [Daly et al., *Validation of an advective-deterministic approach to short wave breaking in a surf-beat model* (2012)](https://svn.oss.deltares.nl/repos/xbeach/Courses/DSD_2015/Documents/Literature/Daly_etal_2012.pdf)
- A full 3D method buys physical crest structure, but not automatically visual foam. Research on particle fluid rendering adds a distinct foam representation and layered rendering to show volumetric water and foam. [Bagar, Scherzer & Wimmer, *A Layered Particle-Based Fluid Model for Real-Time Rendering of Water* (2010)](https://www.cg.tuwien.ac.at/research/publications/2010/bagar2010/)

## Implementation path and remaining checks

1. **Shared break state:** the existing `breakingAt(x,z)` and crest position spawn the 3D sheet, lip brightness, and spray; surface foam retains a short decaying history and clears on replay.
2. **One bounded lip mesh:** connected parcel layers now move forward and down under gravity and render a thickened overhang. Collision queries the same parcels. Keep the pocket visually readable; a full tube remains future work. The connected-sheet precedent is [Thürey et al. (2007)](https://matthias-research.github.io/pages/publications/breakingWaves.pdf).
3. **Budgeted whitewater and spray:** use pooled buffer geometry or instanced quads/points, capped by a fixed count; avoid one scene object per droplet. Update only active ranges, set modified attributes' `needsUpdate`, and inspect renderer draw calls and frame time on desktop and mobile. Three.js documents dynamic `BufferAttribute` updates, `PointsMaterial` point rendering, and `WebGLRenderer.info.render.calls`. These APIs establish a low-overhead route, but no source guarantees a particular FPS on the user's device. [Three.js BufferAttribute](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/BufferAttribute.html), [PointsMaterial](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/PointsMaterial.html), [WebGLRenderer](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/WebGLRenderer.html)
4. **Test the seam:** compare parcel positions used by rendering and collision, reset determinism, board response, and frame rate. Add a low-detail mode if measured frame time exceeds the game's target. A future full barrel still needs a decision about volumetric collision and flow before changing the bulk-water solver.

Implementation update: the old visual-only five-row lip has been replaced by a bounded 3D parcel sheet with a thickened mesh and rider contact. The field-driven surface still keeps decaying foam memory, and a bounded spray pool emits soft particles from the active break. The capsule contact region is a proxy for the visible sheet; this does not claim to resolve plunging-wave pressure or reforming surf.
