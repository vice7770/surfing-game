## The "needs physics" list (for phase 5, with the owner)

1. **The lean into the bottom turn before the rail bites** (step 2): the drawn lean follows the physics' bank; an earlier lean is the physics' body's.
2. **The extension climbing** (step 5): the pelvis rising after Compress (still at 0.62 of the crouch 0.3 s on, the knees 82–111° where 0–30° is the target).
3. **The weight back climbing and at the top** (steps 5, 7): 0.53–0.55 (flat water) and 0.65 forward (the real wave) against 70/30 back; the cutback's 0.45–0.48 against 65–70 % back. Pinned in `stanceTargets.test.ts` as phase-5 misses.
4. **The redirect off a face** (step 7): steering onto the heels with the weight back after a bottom turn spins the board on onto the toes on the plane face and nearly throws the rider; on flat water the snaps and cutbacks stall (the deep U, open since 2026-09-27).
5. **The balance coming out of the redirect** (step 8): the physics' alarm 0.6–0.8 through and after it, which the drawn arms show (the counterweight cue).

## "Drawable but not done"

- **The head turning before the board** (step 4): it needs the steer, which only the local rider has (the plan keeps the drawn state off the wire).
- **The climb's look on a wave** (step 6): drawn, but not seen in this check; the owner's ride shows it.
