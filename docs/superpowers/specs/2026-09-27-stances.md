# Stances and the bottom turn: specification

Status: **agreed** in a grilling session with the user on 2026-09-27, after the take-off work (PR #43), and confirmed ("confirm, go ahead with the plan"). The [gameplay milestone spec](2026-09-26-gameplay-milestone.md)'s principles hold: physical inputs, not scripted moves; the player gives intent and the rider's reflexes balance; outcomes are checks, never tuned into passing.

## What a skill is

A skill (the bottom turn, the top turn, the cutback, the off-the-lip, vertical manoeuvres and more) is a real-life manoeuvre that results from doing the right movements in the right order. The game makes those movements physically possible; people judge how well they were done. The game does not score skills for now.

The sequence the user described: catch the wave → stand up → **crouch** on the drop (Shift) → **Compress** at the bottom with a lean → release to extend up the face → weight back (S) for the top turn at the lip → cutback, off-the-lip.

## The two stances

1. **The crouch** (Shift, unchanged: "works as I want"): the stable, prepared stance of the drop. Its depth range already maps onto the reference: a crouch of 0.6 bends the knee to about 106° (the preparation phase's 90–110°).
2. **Compress** (new), the sharp-turn stance at the base of the bottom turn:
   - buttons: **Space** while standing (it paddles lying down), the gamepad's **right trigger** (analog, as the left trigger is for the crouch), a **touch button** beside Crouch; rebindable;
   - the posture: **full crouch depth** (knees and hips at or under 90°, about 84° at full depth), **weight over the front foot**, and the **inside hand reaching toward the water** — frontside the rear arm, backside the leading arm, with toe or heel pressure to match; the hand **drags when the lean brings it to the water** (the reference's pivot); E stays the hand in the wave face;
   - steering stays the player's: the arrows set the lean;
   - it works on its own or held on top of Shift; releasing it extends back to the crouch or to standing, and the extension still pumps; weight back for the top turn stays on S;
   - Enter lies down straight from Compress;
   - the pocket reflex stands aside while compressing;
   - the differences come from the posture itself: low and centred is steady, compressed and leaning turns hard.

## The reference

**de Sousa (2022)**, *Development of an observation tool for the Bottom-Turn manoeuvre in surfers*, master's thesis, Faculdade de Motricidade Humana, Universidade de Lisboa (a deterministic model and critical features validated with seven national coaches). Its phases:
- **Preparation:** the head toward the bottom of the wave; the trunk over the front foot; knees flexed 90–110°.
- **Fundamental (the turn):** the head turns toward the lip; knees and hips flexed to 90° or less; the body leans into the curve until the inside hand nears the water; the rail engaged to about two thirds of its length.
- **Final (extension):** the inside hand leaves the water and the trunk starts rotating; knees (to 150° or more) and hips extend progressively; the rail is released; the chest and the leading arm point at the lip.
- Weight: over the front foot while flexed, moving to the back foot while extending. The line: a deep U that keeps the speed and places the surfer at the critical part of the wave.

The flow the user pointed to: "Snap In The Lip Over Shallow Reef" (Surfers of Bali, YouTube short `xTgYU5ShueI`): trim along the face, compress into the bottom turn, extend up to the lip, snap with the trunk twisting and the arm up, compress back down.

The reference governs the stances' physics (depths, weight), Part B's animation (head, trunk rotation, arms, knees) and the Surf School's bottom-turn lesson. It is not a score.

## Physics targets

- **The crouched mid-turn wobble** (a crouch deepened during a full-steer turn at about 10 m/s wobbles in yaw at about 2.3 Hz) is fixed as part of Compress, tested at 7–11 m/s.
- **The deep U:** a compressed, full-lean bottom turn from the bottom of the face at 7–8 m/s reaches about 90–100° in about 1 s and keeps at least about 85 % of its speed (Forsyth et al. 2024: 99° in 0.96 s at 1.9 rad/s; turn flow 0.88–0.95). Today's bottom turns reach about 65°.

## Order of work

1. Compress in the physics, with the wobble fixed and the deep-U test.
2. The Regular/Goofy setting (default Regular).
3. The Surf School's bottom-turn lesson teaches the sequence and passes when it is done in order (crouch on the drop, compress at the bottom with a lean, release up the face).
4. Learning Compress: a line on the Controls screen, a one-time hint the first time the player stands in Practice, and the lesson.
5. Part B animation from the reference's cues (head to the lip, trunk rotating, leading arm pointing, knees), other surfers online included.
6. The top turn and the cutback checked against the video (the snap: weight back, trunk twist, arm up).
7. Off-the-lip and vertical manoeuvres later.
