# Take-off on the Face Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The pop-up cue lights when the wave has caught the board high on the face, so a rider who pops up on it (the player or the autopilot) stands on the face instead of the flat ahead of the wave.

**Architecture:** The cue keeps its physical rule (planing down a face) and gains Kimura and Kakinuma's take-off window read from the wave-frame gauge: the board keeping pace with the crest, high on the face, close behind the crest. The runner, which owns the gauge, publishes the combined cue in the status and the snapshot; the ride report's autopilot view reads the same function.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vitest, the rolldown-built report scripts.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-27-riding-the-wave.md` (the reference wave's done criteria; its Findings trace the short rides to the take-off) and the gameplay milestone spec's P10 ("pop-up attempts can be early or late and are recoverable; the physics decides").

## The evidence

Traced on the reference wave (the Canyon, Practice, Hs 1.4 m; riding-the-wave plan, Findings):
- The cue lit with the board already 4.3–5.4 m ahead of the crest, at 22–46 % of the face, moving 7.4–8.6 m/s. The pop-up takes 1.25 s (Borgonovo-Santos et al. 2021), so riders stood 8.4 m ahead of the crest with the face under them flat (median of 8 traced take-offs).
- The moment the wave catches the board comes earlier: 2.6–3 m ahead of the crest, 77–83 % up the face, the board at 4.7–5.4 m/s against a crest at 6.5 m/s. There planing pressure carries only 1–4 % of board and rider (the face's water runs shoreward with the wave, so the board moves slowly through it), against the cue's 35 %.
- Kimura and Kakinuma 2015, which the cue already cites, frame the take-off by the board's speed relative to the crest and its place on the face.

## Global Constraints

- The pop-up's duration stays the lab's 1.2 s (Borgonovo-Santos et al. 2021): not tuned.
- The cue is a hint, never an action: popping up stays the player's (the physics decides what follows).
- No forces added; only when the cue lights changes.
- Performance is never a gate.

## Review Focus

1. Lying on a flat sea or behind the crest, the new cue stays dark (no valid face; the crest ahead of the board).
2. Standing, landing or fallen, the cue stays dark.

## Findings

**Built:** the take-off window (`src/physics/takeOffCue.ts`), and the runner's cue lighting in it as well as on the rider's planing rule, in the status the HUD reads, the snapshot and the ride report's autopilot view.

**Two fixes found by tracing, each with a test that failed first:**
- The gauge's crest speed reads near 0 for a moment when it picks up a new crest, and the first window opened on paddlers at 1.8–2.5 m/s at the crest (they stood behind it). Caught now means the board and the crest both move at least 3 m/s (a paddler sprints at 1.5–1.9 m/s; small waves run about 3 m/s).
- Of fourteen traced early pop-ups, those begun within 2 m of the crest mostly failed or were kicked out, while those from 2–4 m ahead stood on the face. The window opens 2–4 m ahead of the crest.

**The take-off now** (26 traced pop-ups on the reference wave): riders who stand do so on the face, a median 2.3 m ahead of the crest and 82 % up it at 6.7 m/s (before: 8.4 m ahead on the flat). 12 of the 26 fell during the pop-up: 7 by a hard landing ("impact", the contact's 4 body-weight limit) and 4 by losing the board, on the bigger, faster waves (crests at 6–8.6 m/s), where popping up high on the face becomes an air drop. The crest was not breaking at any of them.

**The reference wave's done criteria** (two seeds × 5 min, S-turns with the reflex; [ride report](../../research/ride-report-practice.md)):

| Criterion | Target | Before this plan | Now |
|---|---|---|---|
| Median ride | ≥ 10 s | 2.6 s | 3.0 s |
| Best rides | 15–20 s | 4.7 s | 6.4 s |
| Rides ≥ 3 s | | 19 of 51 stands | 26 of 51 |
| Lost the wave | 0 | 5 | 2 |
| Mean speed | 6–9 m/s | 4.8 m/s | 6.7 m/s |

The mean speed now sits inside the spec's range and the bottom turns match Forsyth's rate, speed, radius, load and rail (not yet their 99° of yaw). Still open: the rides end in falls (all 26 the analyzer read), the pop-up on bigger waves (P10's late take-offs and landing), re-catching a slow board, the crouched wobble at speed, and the curl's distance (the peel).
