# P4d Rider on the Board Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A separate 73 kg rider on the P4c board, the rider of the [board and surfer plan](../../research/board-surfer-physics-plan.md) §3.2 and §5 (B2). The rider lies prone and paddles, pops up by player command, stands and steers by shifting weight. A failed or overloaded contact separates the rider into `main`'s `DetachedSurfer` with continuous momentum. It runs in the worker, and the physical mode shows it.

**Architecture:**
- **Posture.** The rider is a rigid posture of the seven `DetachedSurfer` parts (same masses and volumes), placed in the board frame for each phase: prone, push, landing, standing, recover.
  - A critically damped filter moves the posture toward its target: the phase pose, plus a steering lean and a balance correction.
  - Regular and goofy stances mirror each other across the stringer.
- **Coupling.** While the contact holds, the rider moves with the board and the posture.
  - The rider joins the board's 6 × 6 implicit solve as a mass point with its own inertia, driven by the posture's velocity: one composite body.
  - The contact impulse follows from the solve. It is checked against what a body standing or lying on a deck can give:
    - push only;
    - a line of action through the rider's centre of mass that meets the deck inside the phase's support region (feet, or chest and hands);
    - a friction cone;
    - a leg-load cap.
  - An impulse outside that set is projected onto it. The board and rider are then re-solved apart, so the rider tips, slips or flies instead of receiving grip that does not exist.
  - Posture error then grows or recovers. Beyond a recoverable limit, or after too long in flight, the rider separates.
- **Water on the rider.** Each part samples the water through `SurfWater`, with buoyancy and drag as in `DetachedSurfer`.
- **Paddling.** Alternating hand strokes drag against the local water beside the rails.
- **Separation.** `DetachedSurfer.start` takes the rider's centre of mass, velocity and the board's angular velocity. The runner then steps the detached surfer on `SurfWaterBodyField` and resolves its board contacts.
- **Worker and view.**
  - Advance requests carry the player's input: paddle, pop-up, steer, retry.
  - Snapshots carry the rider's phase and seven part positions.
  - `PhysicalMode` draws `Surfer` through `updateDetached` for every phase, with its legacy board hidden, and a ride camera follows the board.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, three.js math, Vitest.

## Sources (checked 2026-09-25)

- **Borgonovo-Santos et al. 2021** (laboratory pop-up, 23 surfers):
  - 1.20 ± 0.19 s in total, about 60 % push and 40 % landing;
  - hands 0.46 m apart and feet 0.63 m apart;
  - peak landing force 1.63 ± 0.18 body weights, 72/28 front/rear.

  These are a plausibility envelope, not tuned thresholds.
- **Shormann & in het Panhuis 2020:** the 73 kg reference rider (P4b).
- **Sheppard et al. 2012 and 2013; Volschenk et al. 2021:** prone paddling technique and stroke phases. The plan's 1.5–2 m/s sustained paddling speed stays **provisional**.
- **Kimura & Kakinuma 2015:** a catch needs crest-relative speed and a position on the face. This informs the opportunity cue only.

## Modelling choices (not measured, stated)

- **Stance:** the rear foot 0.30 m from the tail, over the fins, and the front foot 0.63 m ahead of it.
- **Postures:** these centre-of-mass heights above the deck are illustrative, not measured:

  | Posture | COM height | Part positions |
  |---|---|---|
  | Standing crouch | 0.82 m | |
  | Landing crouch | 0.62 m | |
  | Push | 0.40 m | hands on the rails |
  | Prone | 0.12 m | chest over the front third, legs trailing past the tail |

- **Support regions:**
  - standing: from the rear foot − 6 cm to the front foot + 6 cm, and ±0.13 m across (feet across the board);
  - push: chest to hands, ±0.23 m;
  - prone: ±0.55 m along, ±0.18 m across.
- **Contact limits:** foot friction μ 0.9 and prone μ 0.7; leg load at most 4 body weights; flight at most 0.4 s; recoverable posture error 0.25 m.
- **Posture response:** ω = 12 rad/s. Steering lean up to 0.25 m at the centre of mass. The balance correction moves the centre of mass toward the required centre of pressure, with τ = 0.15 s, capped at 0.35 m across and 0.25 m along.
- **Paddle stroke:** an 1.1 s cycle per arm, alternating, with the hand pulling back beside the rail 0.25 m below the deck. Hand and forearm area 0.05 m², C_d 1.1. The pull speed is calibrated once for a sustained flat-water speed of about 1.7 m/s, and recorded.
- **Pop-up:** minimum-jerk paths between postures, 0.72 s push then 0.48 s landing. Standing counts once the feet are down, the board is not sinking (deck at the feet no more than 0.1 m under the surface, and sinking slower than 0.3 m/s), and the centre of pressure is inside the feet. Otherwise the rider returns to prone while the board still carries them.
- **Opportunity cue:**
  - planing pressure carries at least half the weight;
  - speed is at least 2 m/s;
  - the surface falls at least 2° along the heading.

  It shows and never triggers.
- **Separation pose:** `DetachedSurfer.start` rebuilds its own standing layout around the handed-over centre of mass. Momentum is continuous, but the limbs snap. A `startFromPose` in the surfer's own file is the fix, proposed to its owner.

## Tasks

### Task 1: Rider posture and board coupling
- `src/physics/riderPosture.ts`: stances, phase poses (seven parts, masses and volumes matching `DetachedSurfer`), support regions, mirroring.
- `src/physics/AttachedRider.ts`: state, posture filter, and the coupling hooks `BoardBody` calls each substep:
  - add the composite mass and drive;
  - check the impulse and project it;
  - integrate the rider.
- `BoardBody` gains an optional rider, keeps the board and rider energy ledgers apart (the contact work on both sides), and exposes contact diagnostics:
  - the impulse and centre of pressure;
  - the foot loads;
  - the support margin.
- Tests:
  - A standing rider towed at 6 m/s is carried: the contact load equals the weight, and the centre of pressure stays between the feet.
  - Goofy mirrors regular.
  - Standing on a board at rest in flat water sinks.
  - The rider's and board's momentum and energy close with the water's work.
  - The part masses and volumes equal `DetachedSurfer`'s.
- Commit `feat: couple a rider to the board through checked contacts`.

### Task 2: Prone rider in the water, and paddling
- Buoyancy and drag on the rider's parts, whose moments pass to the board while attached. Alternating paddle strokes.
- Tests:
  - A prone rider floats on the board with the chest above water.
  - Paddling in flat water reaches a steady 1.5–2 m/s (record the calibrated pull speed) and never planes.
  - A following current raises the speed over ground.
  - Strokes stop when the hands leave the water.
- Commit `feat: float and paddle a prone rider`.

### Task 3: Pop-up
- The phases push → landing → standing, or recover to prone. The support switches from chest and hands to feet. Diagnostics: duration, landing peak in body weights, front/rear split. The opportunity cue.
- Tests:
  - On a towed planing board the pop-up stands in 1.2 s, with a landing peak of 1.2–2.0 BW that loads the front foot more.
  - At rest in flat water it fails and returns to prone.
  - The cue is off in flat water and on when planing down a face.
  - Paddling stops at the push.
- Commit `feat: pop up from prone to standing by command`.

### Task 4: Weight-shift steering and separation
- Steering maps to a lean target, mirrored by stance. The balance correction. Separation causes, handed to `DetachedSurfer`:
  - balance;
  - foot slip;
  - lost board;
  - impact.
- Tests:
  - Opposite steering gives opposite roll and lateral drift.
  - Regular and goofy mirror each other.
  - A sudden stop throws the rider forward and separates them, with linear momentum continuous to 1e-9.
  - The board keeps floating afterwards.
  - Assists are absent, so no force differs.
- Commit `feat: steer by weight shift and separate on lost support`.

### Task 5: The rider in the worker and the physical mode
- Input rides on advance requests.
- The runner carries the rider, the detached surfer after separation, and a retry that relaunches both in the lineup.
- Snapshots carry the rider's phase and seven part positions. The worker and in-page snapshots stay bit-identical.
- `PhysicalMode` draws the rider, maps paddle, pop-up and steer keys, and adds a ride camera.
- Browser check: paddle, pop up on a Beach wave, fall and retry.
- Commit `feat: ride the physical surf zone`.
