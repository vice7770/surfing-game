# Compress: the Second Stance Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A second standing stance, Compress, lets the player make the bottom turn the way surfers do (crouch on the drop, compress at the bottom with a lean, release up the face), with the Regular/Goofy setting, the Surf School teaching the sequence, and the physics targets met or traced.

**Architecture:** Compress is a new rider input like the crouch: a posture (full crouch depth, weight over the front foot, the inside hand reaching for the water) the physics already knows how to carry; its drop into depth is driven by the turn's load so it can be taken mid-turn. Bindings, hints and the Controls screen follow the crouch's pattern; the stance setting reaches the rider through the runner's options; the Surf School gains a sequence goal.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, three.js, Vitest, the rolldown-built report scripts.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-27-stances.md` (reference: de Sousa 2022, the bottom-turn observation tool). Part B's animation (spec item 5) and the top turn and cutback checks (items 6–7) follow in later plans.

## Global Constraints

- Physical inputs, not scripted moves: Compress changes the posture only (depth, weight, the hand); no added forces, no automatic lean. Steering stays the arrows.
- Shift's crouch is unchanged ("works as I want"): its drop rate, depth and hold stay as they are.
- Compress: Space standing (paddle lying down keeps it), gamepad RT (analog; RT keeps paddling lying down), a touch button beside Crouch; rebindable. Works alone or over Shift. Enter lies down from it. The pocket reflex stands aside while compressing. E stays the wave-face hand.
- Regular/Goofy: a Gameplay setting, default Regular.
- Skills are not scored; the Surf School lesson checks the order of the movements, as lessons already pass on what the player did.
- Performance is never a gate. English only; strings in `src/ui/strings.ts`.

## Review Focus

1. Compress held while lying down, landing or fallen does nothing (the crouch's context rule: standing only).
2. Compress and E together: E's wave-face hand wins; the reaching hand never doubles the drag.
3. Releasing Compress while Shift is held returns to the Shift crouch's depth, not to standing.
4. Changing Regular/Goofy mid-session takes effect on the next ride, never mid-ride.
5. A lesson attempt that compresses before crouching, or never releases, does not pass.

### Task 1: Compress in the rider

**Files:** `src/physics/RideSession.ts` (`RideInput.compress`), `src/physics/AttachedRider.ts` (`compress`, the leg's rest depth, the weight), `src/game/pocketReflex.ts` (stand aside); tests in `src/physics/AttachedRider.test.ts`, `src/game/pocketReflex.test.ts`.

**Interfaces:** Produces `RideInput.compress?: number` (0–1, standing), `AttachedRider.compress = 0`. Depth: the leg's rest follows `max(crouch, compress)`. Weight: the fore/aft trim target is `clamp(trim + COMPRESS_WEIGHT · compress, −1, 1)` with `COMPRESS_WEIGHT = 0.5` (the reference: weight over the front foot while flexed). The reflex returns the input unchanged while `compress > 0`.

### Task 2: The reaching hand

**Files:** `src/physics/AttachedRider.ts` (`waveSide` → the hand's side; `standingHand` → the hand's place along the board); tests in `src/physics/AttachedRider.test.ts`.

**Interfaces:** While compressing (`compress ≥ REACH_FROM`, 0.5) and banked at least `REACH_LEAN` (10°) with no E, the hand side is the side the body leans toward (`sign(bank)`), without E's bend of the lean. The hand sits `REACH_ALONG` (0.3 m) along the board toward the arm it belongs to: the arm on the +x side is the leading (left) arm for Regular and the rear one for Goofy (Regular faces the −x rail), so the offset is `side · (regular ? +1 : −1) · REACH_ALONG` — frontside (toward the toes) the rear arm reaches back, backside the leading arm reaches forward. Its drag is E's (`standingHand`), acting only where the hand is under the surface.

- [ ] Failing tests: compressing into a full-lean turn on the 15° face at 7 m/s, after 0.6 s the drawn hand on the lean side sits at or below the water and toward the tail for a Regular frontside turn (toward the nose backside, mirrored for Goofy); without compress there is no reaching hand; with E held the hand is E's (the wave side) and the bank reference carries E's bend only then.
- [ ] Implement; run the rider tests; commit.

### Task 3: Compress taken mid-turn holds (the wobble)

The evidence (take-off plan, ledger and memory `p4e-carve-root-cause`): a crouch deepened during a full-steer turn at about 10 m/s wobbles in yaw at about 2.3 Hz; the crouch's hold stops the fall but causes the wobble; scaling the balance's gains, the leg's softening and hysteresis on the hold did not help. Compress is exactly a deepening mid-turn.

**Files:** `src/physics/AttachedRider.ts` (the leg's rest rate while compressing); tests in `src/physics/AttachedRider.test.ts`.

**Interfaces:** While `compress > crouch`, the rest's downward acceleration is limited by the turn's load instead of the crouch's fixed 0.6 g and its hold: `max(0, f_leg − COMPRESS_KEEP · g)`, where `f_leg` is the specific force along the leg (about g on flat water, more in a turn) and `COMPRESS_KEEP = 0.9` (the feet keep at least 0.9 of the rider's weight). Surfers compress under a turn's load: slowly as the rail sets, quickly once it loads them up.

- [ ] Failing test: on flat water at 8, 10 and 11 m/s, full steer for 0.5 s, then `compress = 1` held: the rider stays on for 1.5 s, the yaw rate changes sign at most twice, and the turn keeps turning (at least 60°, 55° and 45°).
- [ ] Run it and read the traces (yaw, bank, rail, rest, leg force); if the load-driven drop is not the cause, diagnose under superpowers:systematic-debugging and ledger each hypothesis.
- [ ] Implement; run the rider suite (the crouched full-steer, hard-turn and pumping tests must stay green); commit.
