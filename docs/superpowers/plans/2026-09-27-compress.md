# Compress: the Second Stance Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A second standing stance, Compress, lets the player make the bottom turn the way surfers do (crouch on the drop, compress at the bottom with a lean, release up the face), with the Regular/Goofy setting, the Surf School teaching the sequence, and the physics targets met or traced.

**Architecture:** Compress is a new rider input like the crouch: a posture (full crouch depth, weight over the front foot, the inside hand reaching for the water) the physics already knows how to carry; its drop into depth is driven by the turn's load so it can be taken mid-turn. Bindings, hints and the Controls screen follow the crouch's pattern; the stance setting reaches the rider through the runner's options; the Surf School gains a sequence goal.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, three.js, Vitest, the rolldown-built report scripts.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-27-stances.md` (reference: de Sousa 2022, the bottom-turn observation tool). Part B's animation (spec item 5) and the top turn and cutback checks (items 6–7) follow in later plans.

## Global Constraints

- Physical inputs, not scripted moves: Compress changes the posture only (depth, weight, the hand); no added forces, no automatic lean. Steering stays the arrows.
- Shift's crouch is unchanged ("works as I want"): its drop rate, depth and hold stay as they are.
- Compress: Space standing (paddle lying down keeps it), gamepad RT (analog; RT keeps paddling lying down), a touch button beside Crouch; rebindable. Works alone or over Shift. Enter lies down from it. The pocket reflex stands aside while compressing. E stays the wave-face hand.
- Regular/Goofy: a Gameplay setting, default Regular.
- Skills are not scored; the Surf School lesson checks the order of the movements, as lessons already pass on what the player did.
- Performance is never a gate. English only; strings in `src/ui/strings.ts`.

## Review Focus

1. Compress held while lying down, landing or fallen does nothing (the crouch's context rule: standing only).
2. Compress and E together: E's wave-face hand wins; the reaching hand never doubles the drag.
3. Releasing Compress while Shift is held returns to the Shift crouch's depth, not to standing.
4. Changing Regular/Goofy mid-session takes effect on the next ride, never mid-ride.
5. A lesson attempt that compresses before crouching, or never releases, does not pass.

### Task 1: Compress in the rider

**Files:** `src/physics/RideSession.ts` (`RideInput.compress`), `src/physics/AttachedRider.ts` (`compress`, the leg's rest depth, the weight), `src/game/pocketReflex.ts` (stand aside); tests in `src/physics/AttachedRider.test.ts`, `src/game/pocketReflex.test.ts`.

**Interfaces:** Produces `RideInput.compress?: number` (0–1, standing), `AttachedRider.compress = 0`. Depth: the leg's rest follows `max(crouch, compress)`. Weight: the fore/aft trim target is `clamp(trim + COMPRESS_WEIGHT · compress, −1, 1)` with `COMPRESS_WEIGHT = 0.5` (the reference: weight over the front foot while flexed). The reflex returns the input unchanged while `compress > 0`.

- [ ] Failing tests: a standing rider asked to compress sinks its centre of mass as far as a full crouch (compare with `crouch = 1` after 1 s); compress on top of `crouch = 0.6` goes deeper, and releasing it returns to the 0.6 crouch's height; compressing moves the centre of pressure forward (the contact's centre of pressure `z` rises); `lieDown` works while compressing; `withPocketReflex` returns the input untouched with `compress: 1`.
- [ ] Implement; run `npx vitest run src/physics/AttachedRider.test.ts src/game/pocketReflex.test.ts src/physics/RideSession.test.ts`; commit.

### Task 2: The reaching hand

**Files:** `src/physics/AttachedRider.ts` (`waveSide` → the hand's side; `standingHand` → the hand's place along the board); tests in `src/physics/AttachedRider.test.ts`.

**Interfaces:** While compressing (`compress ≥ REACH_FROM`, 0.5) and banked at least `REACH_LEAN` (10°) with no E, the hand side is the side the body leans toward (`sign(bank)`), without E's bend of the lean. The hand sits `REACH_ALONG` (0.3 m) along the board toward the arm it belongs to: the arm on the +x side is the leading (left) arm for Regular and the rear one for Goofy (Regular faces the −x rail), so the offset is `side · (regular ? +1 : −1) · REACH_ALONG` — frontside (toward the toes) the rear arm reaches back, backside the leading arm reaches forward. Its drag is E's (`standingHand`), acting only where the hand is under the surface.

- [ ] Failing tests: compressing into a full-lean turn on the 15° face at 7 m/s, after 0.6 s the drawn hand on the lean side sits at or below the water and toward the tail for a Regular frontside turn (toward the nose backside, mirrored for Goofy); without compress there is no reaching hand; with E held the hand is E's (the wave side) and the bank reference carries E's bend only then.
- [ ] Implement; run the rider tests; commit.

### Task 3: Compress taken mid-turn holds (the wobble)

The evidence (take-off plan, ledger and memory `p4e-carve-root-cause`): a crouch deepened during a full-steer turn at about 10 m/s wobbles in yaw at about 2.3 Hz; the crouch's hold stops the fall but causes the wobble; scaling the balance's gains, the leg's softening and hysteresis on the hold did not help. Compress is exactly a deepening mid-turn.

**Files:** `src/physics/AttachedRider.ts` (the leg's rest rate while compressing); tests in `src/physics/AttachedRider.test.ts`.

**Interfaces:** While `compress > crouch`, the rest's downward acceleration is limited by the turn's load instead of the crouch's fixed 0.6 g and its hold: `max(0, f_leg − COMPRESS_KEEP · g)`, where `f_leg` is the specific force along the leg (about g on flat water, more in a turn) and `COMPRESS_KEEP = 0.9` (the feet keep at least 0.9 of the rider's weight). Surfers compress under a turn's load: slowly as the rail sets, quickly once it loads them up.

- [ ] Failing test: on flat water at 8, 10 and 11 m/s, full steer for 0.5 s, then `compress = 1` held: the rider stays on for 1.5 s, the yaw rate changes sign at most twice, and the turn keeps turning (at least 60°, 55° and 45°).
- [ ] Run it and read the traces (yaw, bank, rail, rest, leg force); if the load-driven drop is not the cause, diagnose under superpowers:systematic-debugging and ledger each hypothesis.
- [ ] Implement; run the rider suite (the crouched full-steer, hard-turn and pumping tests must stay green); commit.

## Findings

**For the user's decision: Compress turns less than the crouch today.** The spec expects "compressed and leaning turns hard". Measured on the bottom-turn test's trough (7 m/s in, 1.2 s after a full lean, still water):

| Stance | Turn | Speed kept |
|---|---:|---:|
| Standing | 75° | 0.67 |
| Shift's crouch 0.6 | 69° | 0.63 |
| Shift held down (the keyboard's full crouch) | 66° | 0.54 |
| Compress over the crouch 0.6 | 62–64° | 0.48–0.50 |
| Shift then Space (the keyboard's sequence) | 58–60° | 0.53–0.55 |
| Compress alone, from standing | 77–80° | 0.35 |

- The forward weight costs about 6°; the depth the rest.
- On the carve lab's plane face, Compress held over the crouch turns 57° and falls at 1.9 s, while the crouch alone turns 61° and stays on.
- On the reference wave, the autopilot's compressed bottom turns yaw further (87° against 62°) but its rides of 3 s or more halve (26 → 14; the table below).
- The gap is pinned with `it.fails` ("turns at least as hard compressed as crouched"), not tuned. The lesson and the autopilot follow the agreed sequence regardless.
- Options for later:
  - apply the forward weight only low on the face;
  - look at why a deeper body turns less (the lean the turn can hold falls with the speed, and a deeper body sheds more of it);
  - or accept it, with Compress as the stance that absorbs the bottom rather than tightens it.

**Built:**
- Compress: Space standing, the right trigger's travel (a trigger resting under 5 % is no press), and a touch button beside Crouch; rebindable.
  - Full crouch depth, the weight half forward, and the inside hand reaching for the water (frontside the rear arm, backside the leading arm), alone or over Shift.
  - It takes a fresh press standing: Space or RT held from paddling through the pop-up does not compress on landing. Taken over from Shift, it starts at Shift's depth.
  - Enter lies down from it, and the pocket reflex stands aside.
- The Controls line, and a one-time hint in Practice after the crouch's.
- An older save gives Compress its default inputs only where the player's own bindings leave them free.
- The Regular/Goofy setting (Gameplay, Regular by default). A change rides from the next ride: a retry, a lesson restart, or climbing back on after a fall.
- The bottom-turn lesson passes on the sequence in order, in one ride:
  - a crouch without Compress on the drop;
  - Compress with a lean at the bottom (taken higher, the sequence starts over);
  - release climbing;
  - a bottom turn the analysis names after the drop.
- The autopilot's bottom turn follows the same sequence.

**Physics changes, each with a test that failed first:**
- Taken deeper than the crouch, Compress drops only as fast as the turn's load lets the feet keep 0.9 of the rider's weight, without the crouch's hold.
- While compressing, the legs only push: they fold no faster than the body falls onto them. Taken from standing at the trough, the dropping legs had pulled the board up and the rider fell into the turn.
- The reaching hand touches the water rather than plunging. Driven 30 cm under by the fixed arm, it dragged up to 235 N and bled a bottom turn from 7.5 to 4 m/s in 0.3 s.

**The mid-turn wobble is reduced, not gone.** Taken during a full-steer turn on flat water, the yaw rate's largest swing in the next second is 0.8 / 1.3 / 0.9 rad/s at 8 / 10 / 11 m/s.
- With the crouch's hold it was 1.0 / 1.9 / 2.3; the held turn's own swing is 0.7 / 0.7 / 1.0.
- It no longer changes sign, and the rider stays on at 7–11 m/s.
- What remains is the full-lean turn's own lightly damped roll–yaw (the Mode B family), which any posture change mid-turn excites.
- Braking the drop harder, slowing it, and scaling the ankle with the leg did not help.

**The deep U is not met** (pinned with `it.fails`). No stance reaches 90° within 1.2 s keeping 85 % of its speed on still water (the table above).
- A carve at a 40–48° rail sheds about 0.45 g, and the lean the turn can hold falls with the speed.
- Forsyth's turn flow of 0.88–0.95 was measured on waves, whose moving water feeds the turn.

**The reference wave's done criteria** (two seeds × 5 min, S-turns with the reflex, the autopilot compressing at the base of its bottom turns; [ride report](../../research/ride-report-practice.md)), against the take-off plan's run, which is the same autopilot without Compress:

|---|---|---|---|
| Median ride | ≥ 10 s | 3.0 s | 2.2 s |
| Best rides | 15–20 s | 6.4 s | 4.9 s |
| Rides ≥ 3 s | | 26 of 51 stands | 14 of 49 |
| Lost the wave | 0 | 2 | 1 |
| Mean speed | 6–9 m/s | 6.7 m/s | 7.3 m/s |
| Near the curl | ≥ 50 % | 13 % | 15 % |
| Bottom turns | Forsyth: 99° in 0.96 s, 1.9 rad/s, 7.3 m/s, 3.8 m, 1.41 g, 42° rail | 62° in 0.79 s, 2.1 rad/s, 7.8 m/s, 3.7 m, 1.45 g, 45° (41 turns) | 87° in 1.18 s, 2.1 rad/s, 9.3 m/s, 4.2 m, 1.68 g, 55° (15 turns) |
| Speed kept into the top turn | 0.88–0.95 | 0.36 | 0.32 |

Compressed, the autopilot's bottom turns come close to Forsyth's yaw (87° of 99°), but they take longer, on a rail past its 48° bite. The rides that make them are shorter: rides of 3 s or more fell from 26 to 14. This agrees with the stance table above and belongs to the same decision. Until then, the autopilot could go back to crouch-only bottom turns for the reference report (one line in `src/dev/Autopilot.ts`).
