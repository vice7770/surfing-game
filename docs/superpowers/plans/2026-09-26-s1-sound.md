# S1 Sound Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The game sounds like a surf film. Every sound comes from the physics:
- surf roar where the water breaks, lip impacts by their landed volume, whitewater, distant surf and wind;
- the board's rush and rail spray, paddle splashes, the pop-up, a wipeout plunge and underwater bubbles.

Sound is heard from the camera, slowed by slow motion, muffled underwater and under the pause menu, and set in a new Audio tab.

**Architecture:**
- **The worker reports what makes sound.** Each snapshot carries the lip impacts and paddle strokes since the last snapshot, and the breaking roar by along-shore sector. The rest is already in the snapshot or its status: the board's pose, the ride's phase, speed and separation, and the spray count.
- **A pure mapping** (`src/audio/soundMapping.ts`) turns one frame of that data, plus the camera, into target levels, positions and one-shot events. It is unit-tested in Node.
- **A Web Audio engine** (`src/audio/AudioEngine.ts`) plays the targets:
  - buses: Sea, Board and rider, Interface → Master;
  - a muffle filter for the underwater view and the pause menu;
  - panner nodes placed in the world, with the camera as the listener;
  - playback rate from the time scale;
  - Mono by down-mixing the output.
- **Recordings come from a manifest** (`public/assets/audio/sounds.json`). Every sound id also has a synthesised fallback (`src/audio/synth.ts`, pure DSP, tested), used until its recording loads or if it fails.
- **`App` owns the engine.** It creates it on the first click or key, feeds it each frame, ducks it under the pause menu, and plays the interface sounds.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, the Web Audio API, Vitest (Node), macOS `afconvert` for AAC, and the browser pane for checks.

**Spec:** the ROADMAP section "P1 · Sound (S1)" (requirements from the grilling session of 2026-09-26).

## Record (2026-09-26)

Tasks 1–7, 9 and 10 are implemented. Task 8 (CC0 recordings) waited for the user's approval of the downloads; the CC0 candidates were listed in the ROADMAP until 2026-10-03, when the owner chose generated recordings instead (see the next record).

Where the build departed from this plan:
- **The one-shot shaper (Task 9 → Task 3).** The first sound report showed about 170 lip one-shots a second during a set, and a paddle splash on every step of a hand's pull.
  - The per-frame cap alone would have let hundreds of 1.3 s voices overlap.
  - `OneShotShaper` now gathers impulses by kind and 10 m place, releasing each at most every 0.18 s (jets), 0.25 s (rollers) or 0.45 s (strokes), with the energy it gathered.
  - The roar and rail references were raised (ROAR_REF 20, 3 decades; RAIL_FULL 30), so a set has room to swell.
- **The output limiter (Task 6).** Eight roar sectors, the bed and a burst of crashes can sum past full scale.
- **Recordings after the rest (Task 8).** S1 ships on the synthesised sounds, which were planned to be complete on their own, and the recordings follow as their own PR once the downloads are approved.
- **Checked without listening:** nothing was heard in this session (the browser pane was hidden). The engine's state was read in the page instead; the listening is the user's playtest.

## Record (2026-10-03)

Task 8 was done differently. The owner decided to generate the recordings with ElevenLabs Sound Effects instead of downloading CC0 files, so no download approval was needed and `scripts/assets/fetch-sounds.ts` was never written. Seven sounds have two generated candidates each (`licence: 'generated'` in the manifest, which `parseManifest` now accepts beside `CC0`); their prompts, processing and measurements are in `docs/ASSETS.md`, and the result is in the ROADMAP's S1 record. A review the same day sent the six loops and four of the one-shots back for a second pass: the loops were 16 s rings of four takes with a foam-crackle layer in the roars and a measured distance shape in the distant surf. A second review sent every candidate back; the third pass replaced the two roars with single 30 s takes made through the API and added to the engine pools of recordings (round-robin, with pitch and level jitter), a per-candidate `level`, a path for the bubbles outside the underwater muffle, and a low-pass that falls with distance (`variants.ts`, `SoundBank`, `soundMapping.ts`, `AudioEngine`). The credits ran out before the other twelve files could be remade. A fourth pass, with no credits left, remade seven of them from takes in hand: the distant loops as single 30 s takes in stereo, the bubbles loops without their layers or brick-wall low-passes, and three one-shots with the review's processing; `docs/ASSETS.md` lists what is still outstanding. A fifth pass, again without credits, re-encoded the six loops so each wrap is an ordinary AAC frame (one period of a circular packet stream; a plain encode left codec error at the wrap), opened the engine's context at the recordings' 44.1 kHz so a browser never resamples a loop as it decodes it, and checked the third pass's engine changes against the constraints below: one-shots no longer draw a level at random ("its level comes from a measured quantity"), and the bubbles, which skip the water's muffle, take the pause menu's. The constraint "CC0 only" below describes downloads; generated recordings are used under the owner's ElevenLabs plan, whose terms still need checking before release.

## Global Constraints

- **Nothing scripted:** every sound has a physical cause read from the simulation, and its level comes from a measured quantity. No artificial cues.
- **Assets:** CC0 only, each listed in `docs/ASSETS.md` with its source, author and licence, and shipped as AAC `.m4a`. **Downloading any file needs the user's explicit approval first:** list each file's name, source and size, ask once, and wait.
- **Always playable:** the game must sound complete on the synthesised fallbacks alone.
- **Silent where agreed:** the legacy wave is silent, and there is no music.
- **Mapping values are provisional:** the constants that turn physics into loudness are marked `provisional (by ear)` and tuned in the user's listening playtest.
- **Performance** is measured and never a gate. Voices are capped (32 one-shots at once, the quietest dropped first) for correctness, not speed.
- **P8 files change only to add:** the Audio tab, Mono, the mute action, the speaker toggles, and the per-frame hook.
- **Text:** new player-facing text goes in `src/ui/strings.ts`.
- **Commits:** the repo's conventional style, with the `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>` trailer.

## Review Focus

1. **No context yet, or a suspended one** (autoplay policy, a hidden tab, iOS): the game runs silently without errors, and sound starts at the first gesture (test in Task 5, on the engine's state machine).
2. **Bursts of lip impacts in a big set:** hundreds of landings in one frame must not create hundreds of voices. They merge per sector and frame and are capped at 32 (test in Task 3).
3. **A recording that fails to load or decode** falls back to its synthesised sound, with no silence and no exception (test in Task 4).
4. **Slow motion and pause:** the time scale changes playback rate without restarting loops, and a paused game makes no new one-shots (test in Task 3).
5. **Settings from an older save**, without an `audio` tab: they load with its defaults (test in Task 2).

**Interfaces:**
- Produces, on `SurfZoneBuffers`:
  - `lipHits: Float32Array` (`LIP_HIT_STRIDE = 5`: x, z, volume m³, impact speed m/s, 0) and `lipHitCount`: the lip landings since the last fill. More than `SOUND_EVENT_CAPACITY = 64` are merged into the nearest earlier hit, summing the volume and keeping the highest speed.
  - `strokeHits: Float32Array` (`STROKE_HIT_STRIDE = 4`: x, z, work J = |impulse| × hand speed, 0) and `strokeHitCount`: an attached paddler's strokes since the last fill, capped the same way.
  - `roar: Float32Array` (`ROAR_SECTORS = 8` × 3: power, x, z). The window's along-shore span is split into 8 sectors. The power is Σ B·|q| × cell area over the sector's cells (m⁴/s, provisional as a loudness measure), and x, z are the power-weighted centroid.
- The runner collects hits from `simulation.lipImpacts` and `session.rider.strokes` in `afterWater`, every step, into accumulators that `fill` drains.

- [ ] **Step 1: Failing tests:**

  - A lip landing forced through `simulation.lip.onLand` shows up once in the next fill, then not in the one after.
  - More than 64 landings between fills keep the total volume (within 1e-6).
  - A paddler paddling for 1 s fills `strokeHits` with positive work.
  - The worker round-trip test sees the new buffers transferred.

### Task 3: The physics-to-sound mapping

**Files:**
- Create: `src/audio/soundMapping.ts`, `src/audio/soundMapping.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:

```ts
export interface SoundFrame {
  /** Seconds of real time since the last frame, and the simulation's time scale (0.4–1). */
  dt: number; timeScale: number; paused: boolean;
  listener: { x: number; y: number; z: number; underwater: boolean };
  roar: Float32Array;            // ROAR_SECTORS × (power, x, z)
  lipHits: Float32Array; lipHitCount: number;
  strokeHits: Float32Array; strokeHitCount: number;
  significantHeight: number;     // the swell's Hs, m
  windSpeed: number;             // local wind, m/s (+ onshore)
  board?: { x: number; y: number; z: number; speed: number; sideslip: number }; // sideslip: m/s across the board
  ride?: { phase: RiderPhase; previousPhase: RiderPhase; speed: number };
}
export type LoopId = 'roar' | 'distant' | 'wind' | 'rush' | 'rail' | 'bubbles';
export type OneShotId = 'lipJet' | 'lipRoller' | 'paddle' | 'popUp' | 'plunge';
export interface SoundTargets {
  loops: { id: LoopId; key: string; gain: number; rate: number; position?: { x: number; y: number; z: number } }[];
  oneShots: { id: OneShotId; gain: number; rate: number; position: { x: number; y: number; z: number } }[];
  muffle: number;                // 0 clear … 1 fully muffled
  playbackRate: number;          // the time scale
}
export function soundTargets(frame: SoundFrame): SoundTargets;
export const ONE_SHOT_CAP = 32;
```

- **The rules** (the constants are `provisional (by ear)`):
  - **Roar:** one loop per sector, `key` `roar:<i>`, gain = `clamp(log10(1 + power / ROAR_REF) / ROAR_DECADES)`, placed at its centroid.
  - **Distant:** gain from Hs (0 at 0.3 m, 1 at 3 m), not positional.
  - **Wind:** gain from |windSpeed| / 12, and rate 0.9–1.1 with the speed.
  - **Rush:** at the board, gain from speed (0 below 0.5 m/s, 1 at 12 m/s), rate 0.8–1.3 with the speed.
  - **Rail:** at the board, gain from sideslip × speed.
  - **Bubbles:** while the listener is underwater.
  - **Lip hits:** a one-shot each, `lipJet` when the impact speed is at least 4 m/s, otherwise `lipRoller`, with gain from volume × speed².
  - **Strokes:** a `paddle` one-shot each, with gain from the work.
  - **Phase changes:** `prone` → `push` gives `popUp`; any → `fallen` gives `plunge`, with gain from the speed.
  - **The cap:** one-shots are sorted by gain, and only the loudest `ONE_SHOT_CAP` are kept.
  - **Muffle:** 1 when underwater. Paused, 0.85, with the Sea loops held at a quiet bed (gain × 0.25) and no one-shots.
  - **Playback rate** = the time scale.
- [ ] **Step 1: Failing tests**, one per rule. They include:
  - silence on a calm sea with no board;
  - roar louder for a larger power, and placed at the centroid;
  - 200 lip hits give exactly 32 one-shots, the loudest ones (Review Focus 2);
  - a paused frame gives no one-shots and a muffled quiet bed (Review Focus 4);
  - a time scale of 0.4 gives a playback rate of 0.4.
- [ ] **Step 2: Implement, run, commit** `feat: map the physics to sound levels, places and events`.

### Task 4: Synthesised sounds and the manifest

**Files:**
- Create: `src/audio/synth.ts`, `src/audio/synth.test.ts`, `src/audio/SoundBank.ts`, `src/audio/soundManifest.ts`, `src/audio/soundManifest.test.ts`, `public/assets/audio/sounds.json`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `synthesize(id: LoopId | OneShotId | 'click' | 'chime', sampleRate: number, seed?: number): Float32Array`. It is seeded, pure DSP:
    - loops are shaped noise that joins seamlessly at its ends: roar is low-passed brown noise with slow swells; wind is band-passed; rush and rail are high-passed; bubbles are sparse resonant blips;
    - one-shots are short enveloped noise, with jet and plunge given a low thump.
  - `interface SoundManifest { sounds: Record<string, { candidates: { file: string; source: string; author: string; licence: 'CC0' }[]; chosen: number; gain: number }> }` and `parseManifest(raw: unknown): SoundManifest`, which drops invalid entries.
  - `class SoundBank { constructor(context: BaseAudioContext, manifest: SoundManifest); buffer(id): AudioBuffer }`. It returns the synthesised buffer at once, then swaps in the recording once it has loaded and decoded, and keeps the synth if loading fails.
  - `sounds.json` starts with no candidates, so everything is synthesised until Task 8.

  - the same seed gives the same samples;

### Task 5: The audio engine

**Files:**
- Create: `src/audio/AudioEngine.ts`, `src/audio/audioState.ts`, `src/audio/audioState.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `audioState(event, state)`, a pure state machine: `idle` (no context) → `running` on the first gesture, `suspended` when out of view with muting on, back to `running` when visible again. Mute toggles a flag.
  - `class AudioEngine`:
    - `static create(): AudioEngine | undefined` returns undefined without Web Audio;
    - `update(targets: SoundTargets, listenerPose)`: loops keep their source nodes and ramp their gain (τ 0.08 s), rate and position; one-shots start new sources;
    - `setVolumes(audio: AudioSettings)` and `setMono(on)`;
    - `setMuted(on)`, `playUi('click' | 'chime')`, `resume()` and `suspend()`.
  - The graph: every source → its panner (positional sounds, HRTF, inverse distance, reference 8 m, rolloff 1) → its bus gain (Sea, Board and rider, Interface). The world buses → a muffle low-pass (1 → 18 kHz, fully muffled → 400 Hz) → Master → destination. Interface sounds bypass the muffle. Mono sets `destination.channelCount = 1` (explicit).
  - On Safari 16.4 and later, `navigator.audioSession.type = 'ambient'`, so the silent switch mutes the game.
- [ ] **Step 1: Failing tests** for `audioState`: every transition, a gesture before any context, a hidden tab with muting off, and mute while suspended (Review Focus 1).
- [ ] **Step 2: Implement.** The engine is browser-only: it is checked in the browser pane in Task 6 and needs no Node test.
- [ ] **Step 3: Commit** `feat: play the sound through a Web Audio engine`.
