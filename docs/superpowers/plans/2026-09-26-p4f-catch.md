# P4f Catch Calibration, Practice and Natural Modes Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make catching a physical wave possible at real speeds on the stage 2 surf zone, with the same force laws in a practice mode (controlled groundswell that keeps rideable faces coming) and the natural mode (seeded sea states), and report both (board plan B4; wave plan §1.10; validation protocol §A–B).

**Architecture:**
- **No new forces.** A catch stays emergent: paddling, the face's slope and orbital flow, planing lift, and the pop-up's checked contacts.
- **Calibrated:** only the modelling choices the P4d and P4e records name as such:
  - the stand checks: feet depth and sinking rate;
  - the prone hold;
  - the pop-up cue.

  Each is recorded with its evidence (plan §7: source, conditions, measured or playtested).
- **Practice mode** changes only the incoming water: a narrow-band, narrow-spread groundswell. It restarts the rider without stopping the wave.
- **Measuring:** a ghost-rider tool runs many riders over one surf zone, reactions ignored, to measure catch statistics per start position.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vitest, the stage 2 surf zone.

**Spec:**
- [Board and surfer physics plan](../../research/board-surfer-physics-plan.md) (practice and natural modes, B4);
- [validation protocol](../../research/board-surfer-validation-protocol.md);
- [wave formation plan](../../research/wave-formation-plan.md) §1.10.

## Findings that shape it (2026-09-26)

- **The bot on stage 2 at the default sea** (Hs 1.4 m, Tp 10 s, 2 seeds × 3 minutes per spot, waiting 3 m outside the break line): 135 attempts, 6 cues, 0 stood. 51 attempts ended with the prone rider lifted off ('lost board'). The Canyon's crests never reached the bot's trigger.
- **Faces:** stage 2 faces reach 15–25° thousands of times a minute on the Point and Reef (up to 41–45°). Catchable faces exist.
- **Ghost riders on the Point** (30 riders over five positions along shore and six across the break line, 1.5 minutes): 355 attempts, 30 cues, 3 stood (rides of 1.2, 1.4 and 8.2 s). Catches are possible, but cues rarely convert.
- **Why cues fail**, of 30:

  | Outcome | Count |
  |---|---:|
  | Refused: board sinking into the surface | 7 |
  | Refused: feet more than 0.1 m under water | 7 |
  | Fell while rising (balance) | 5 |
  | Lifted off while rising (lost board) | 4 |
  | Refused: contact strained | 4 |
  | Stood | 3 |

- **Pop-up policy is not the lever.** Popping at 50 % or 70 % planing, or at 5–6 m/s on a face, converts no better than the cue.
- **Static-face envelope** (prone, pop-up after 3 s): a stand needs the board planing at about 4.8 m/s with about half the weight on planing lift (15° faces; 12° from a 5 m/s start). On a wave, the water on the face already runs shoreward at 1–1.6 m/s.
- **Practice-like groundswell** (Hs 2 m, Tp 12 s, narrow band and spread), ghost riders over 2 minutes:
  - Point: 46 cues, 2 stood (4.1 and 4.5 s);
  - Reef: 37 cues, none;
  - Canyon: no trigger.

## Record (2026-09-26)

- **Task 1:** a refused pop-up says why ('strained', 'sinking', 'feet under water', 'no water'), and the Wave Lab says it in the player's words. Paddling on flat water never cues or stands, and a passing swell's face speeds a paddler up.
- **Task 2:** swept with 30 ghost riders on the Point's practice-like groundswell for 1.5 min per setting (Hs 2 m, Tp 12 s, narrow band and spread):

  | Feet limit, m | Sinking limit, m/s | Prone grip, BW | Cues | Stood | Rides ≥ 3 s |

  - **The prone grip was the lever:** at 0.3 body weights a steepening face lifted most paddlers off the board before the cue. At 0.6 (both hands on the rails) cues more than double, and every stand rode at least 3 s.
  - **The feet limit stays at 0.1 m.** From 0.2 m a rider stands on the static 10° face that the P4d tests say is too gentle to plane on. 0.15 m stood more riders but gave no more rides of 3 s.
  - **The sinking limit stays at 0.3 m/s:** 0.8 m/s changed nothing.
  - The P4d pop-up tests and the Task 1 tests pass with the kept values.
- **Task 3:** Practice is a third swell source in the Wave Lab, next to Buoy and Storm: a 2 m, 12 s groundswell with spreading s 40 and a ±8 % band, from 10°. Only the incoming water changes; the solver and forces are the natural mode's. R restarts the rider without stopping the wave.
- **Task 4:** `npm run report:catch` takes `--practice` and `--ghosts` (30 riders along and across the break line; the lip still strikes them, but neither the water nor the lip feels them). Seed 1, 2 minutes per spot:

  [Natural report](../../research/catch-report.md), [practice report](../../research/catch-report-practice.md).
- **What the reports say:**
  - Catches work at real speeds on stage 2 waves: riders stand and ride up to 11.6 s straight in, reaching 10–12 m/s.
  - Most attempts still end prone: the rider is lifted off the board ('lost board') by a face or bore before the cue, most of all for riders waiting inside the break line.
  - The Canyon's crests rarely reach the bots' trigger, and never cue.
- **The 30-second practice ride with a bottom turn and a top turn is not met.** The bots ride straight; the longest ride is 11.6 s. Linking turns needs a peeling spot (the Point and Reef mostly close out, P3b) and the finite-impedance rider for hard carves (P4e).
- **The swimmer climbs back on (surfer plan S3):** after a fall the swimmer strokes with Space and steers with the arrows. Enter within reach of the board grabs it and lies back down prone. Linear momentum is kept across the join.
- **Not done:** retiring `legacy`. Catching works but is not yet reliable, so the legacy wave stays playable.
