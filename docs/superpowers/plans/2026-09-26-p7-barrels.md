# P7 Barrels Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Plunging waves in the physical surf zone throw a continuous, water-conserving lip sheet that forms a tube (or not) from the wave's own state. The rider can be hit by it or covered by it, and a reshaped Reef peels so tubes open progressively.

**Architecture:**
- The depth-averaged Boussinesq solver cannot overturn, so the tube comes from the thrown lip (wave plan §1.9, ADR 0003's approach, ADR 0004 §6).
- A per-column crest tracker measures each crest's speed and its surface water speed from the solver. A column throws a jet when that ratio nears breaking onset on a plunging bed slope.
- The jet leaves at the crest's surface water speed, released over a short time as a strip of parcels. Strips of neighbouring columns thrown close in time are linked into one sheet.
- The rider collides with the sheet's links. The page draws the sheet as a translucent surface.
- The water stays conserved: throws take water from the crest cells, and landings return it.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, three.js, Vitest, the stage 2 surf zone and its worker.

**Spec:** the ROADMAP section "P1 · Barrels (P7)" (requirements from the grilling session of 2026-09-26), the wave formation plan §1.9 and Q12, ADR 0003 and ADR 0004.

## Findings

- **Task 2, 2026-09-26:** the depth-averaged solver cannot give the crest water's speed. At breaking onset on the Reef's edge the crest moved at 4.81 m/s but its depth-averaged water at 2.35 m/s (ratio 0.49), so the 0.85 onset ratio can never be reached. A jet thrown at that speed is overrun by its own wave.
  - The crest's speed is instead measured from the surface's own motion on its front face (c = η_t / |∇η|, within 5 % of the Madsen–Sørensen phase speed), and the jet leaves at that speed (`JET_SPEED_RATIO` = 1, provisional).
  - Even then the lip lands 0–0.16 m ahead of the crest. In a height field, a jet moving with the crest sits on the face beneath it, so no tube opens.
  - A tube needs either a jet faster than the crest, or a jet released from a face gone vertical, falling to the trough ahead. **Both need measured jet kinematics, requested from the user:** the jet's speed relative to the crest at overturning, where it leaves the face, and its thickness. The in-surf tube test is an expected failure until then.

## Global Constraints

- **Physics honest:** no imposed tube shapes, and no tuning a value to force a result. A tube forms or not from the wave's state.
- **Provisional values:** any value without a verified source is marked `provisional` in its doc comment, with the source that should confirm it. The user will supply the jet kinematics, the tube shape against seabed slope, and the crest-speed onset threshold.
- **Water conserved:** exactly, to rounding, through throw, flight and landing (the existing PlungingLip test).
- **Every tier gets tubes:** the sheet is simulated in the worker, on the CPU, and drawn on the page. Budget about 1 ms per step in the worker and about 1 ms on the GPU per frame. If measured above that, record it and optimise later; never fake it.
- **Replays:** CPU replays stay bit-exact, and the worker stays identical to the in-page surf zone.
- **Units and style:** SI units, g = 9.81; doc comments with sources, as in the repo; commit trailer `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
- **Out of scope:** deliberate tube-riding (crouch, stall, trim), the flexible rider, and the rider's speed and position relative to the wave. These are in the ROADMAP's future gameplay list.

## Review Focus

1. **The window slides along shore:** parcels are in world coordinates, but a strip's column index moves with the window. After `shiftAlongShore`, links must still join the right neighbours (test in Task 3).
2. **The parcel pool runs out** during a big set: a throw either releases a whole strip or nothing, never half a strip (test in Task 3).
3. **A crest over dry or very shallow bed** (at low tide, or at the shore) throws nothing and never produces NaN crest speeds (test in Task 1).
4. **The GPU tier:** the tracker and sheet read the solver arrays read back each frame, so they run unchanged. The worker's snapshots carry the larger lip buffer (test in Task 6, the worker transfer test).
5. **A rider under a tube too small for a standing body:** the standing rider is hit and the prone one is only covered (test in Task 4).

### Task 1: Crest kinematics and the throw trigger

**Files:**
- Create: `src/wave/CrestKinematics.ts`, `src/wave/CrestKinematics.test.ts`
- Modify: `src/wave/SurfZoneSimulation.ts` (`markBreakingOnsets`, `throwLip`)

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `surfaceSpeedFactor(kh: number): number` is kh·coth(kh), the linear profile's surface-to-mean speed ratio, capped at `MAX_SURFACE_FACTOR = 3` (provisional).
  - `class CrestTracker(solver: ShallowWaterSolver, peakPeriod: number, firstRow = 0)`, with `update(dt: number): void`, `crest(column: number): CrestState | undefined` and `release(column: number): void`.
  - `interface CrestState { cell: number; speed: number; surfaceSpeed: number; ratio: number; direction: { x: number; z: number } }`.
  - `const BREAKING_ONSET_RATIO = 0.85` (provisional: Barthelemy et al. 2018).
  - `breakerForm(localIribarren: number): 'jet' | 'roller' | 'none'`: 'jet' for 0.4 ≤ ξ ≤ 2 (plunging, Battjes 1974), 'roller' below, 'none' above (surging).
- Consumes: `ShallowWaterSolver` (h, qx, qz, bed, zCenters, rowBelow), and the `peakPeriod` of `SurfZoneConfig`.

The tracker follows one crest per column. It starts from the highest surface over water deeper than 0.05 m, from row `firstRow` shoreward (a constructor option; the surf zone passes `rowBelow(TANK.fineFrom)`). It then follows the local maximum of the surface nearest the crest's predicted position (its last position plus speed × dt, searched within ±3 cells).

It drops the crest, and starts again from the highest, when:
- the column throws;
- the crest runs into water shallower than 0.05 m;
- no local maximum lies within reach.

The crest speed is the smoothed rate of change of its cross-shore position (exponential smoothing over 0.2 s). The surface water speed is the depth-averaged speed at the crest times `surfaceSpeedFactor(kh)`, with k from the peak period at the local depth.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests**

```ts
import { describe, expect, it } from 'vitest';
import { BoussinesqSolver } from './BoussinesqSolver';
import { BREAKING_ONSET_RATIO, CrestTracker, breakerForm, surfaceSpeedFactor } from './CrestKinematics';
import { uniformEdges } from './ShallowWaterSolver';
import { waveNumber } from './dispersion';

describe('crest kinematics', () => {
  it('gives the linear surface-to-mean speed ratio, kh·coth(kh)', () => {
    expect(surfaceSpeedFactor(1e-6)).toBeCloseTo(1, 6);
    expect(surfaceSpeedFactor(1)).toBeCloseTo(1 / Math.tanh(1), 9);
    expect(surfaceSpeedFactor(50)).toBe(3);
  });

  it('measures a linear wave: crest speed near the phase speed, surface-speed ratio near the steepness ak', () => {
    const depth = 10;
    const period = 8;
    const k = waveNumber((2 * Math.PI) / period, depth);
    const amplitude = 0.3;
    const solver = new BoussinesqSolver({ nx: 4, xMin: 0, dx: 1, zEdges: uniformEdges(0, 400, 800), xBoundary: 'periodic' }, () => depth);
    const c = (2 * Math.PI) / period / k;
    for (let iz = 0; iz < solver.nz; iz += 1) {
      const eta = amplitude * Math.cos(k * solver.zCenters[iz]);
      for (let ix = 0; ix < solver.nx; ix += 1) {
        const i = iz * solver.nx + ix;
        solver.h[i] = depth + eta;
        solver.qz[i] = c * eta;
      }
    }
    const tracker = new CrestTracker(solver, period);
    for (let s = 0; s < 120; s += 1) {
      solver.step(1 / 60);
      tracker.update(1 / 60);
    }
    const crest = tracker.crest(1)!;
    expect(crest.speed / c).toBeGreaterThan(0.9);
    expect(crest.speed / c).toBeLessThan(1.1);
    expect(crest.ratio).toBeGreaterThan(0.8 * amplitude * k);
    expect(crest.ratio).toBeLessThan(1.2 * amplitude * k);
  });

  it('has no crest over dry or nearly dry bed', () => {
    const solver = new BoussinesqSolver({ nx: 3, xMin: 0, dx: 1, zEdges: uniformEdges(0, 40, 40) }, () => -1);
    const tracker = new CrestTracker(solver, 10);
    tracker.update(1 / 60);
    expect(tracker.crest(1)).toBeUndefined();
  });

  it('throws a jet only on plunging bed slopes', () => {
    expect(breakerForm(0.2)).toBe('roller');
    expect(breakerForm(0.8)).toBe('jet');
    expect(breakerForm(3)).toBe('none');
    expect(BREAKING_ONSET_RATIO).toBeGreaterThan(0.5);
  });
});
```

### Task 4: Rider contact: hit versus covered

**Files:**
- Modify: `src/wave/PlungingLip.ts` (`forEachContactNear`), `src/physics/AttachedRider.ts` and `src/physics/DetachedSurfer.ts` (use it), `src/physics/RideSession.ts` (`strike`)
- Test: `src/physics/AttachedRider.test.ts`, `src/physics/RideSession.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces: `LipParcelSource.forEachContactNear(center: Vector3, reach: number, visit: (parcel: LipContactParcel) => void): void`. For every link within `reach` of `center`, it offers a virtual parcel:
  - placed at the link's closest point to `center`;
  - with the link's interpolated velocity and previous position;
  - carrying the link's share of volume, with radius the sheet's half thickness (volume per link over the link's area, at least 0.05 m).

  A velocity change the visitor makes is split back to the link's two parcels by their weights, so momentum is conserved.
- Consumes: `forEachLink` from Task 3.
