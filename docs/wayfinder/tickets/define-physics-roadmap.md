# Define the future physics roadmap

Status: RESOLVED  
Type: wayfinder:grilling  
Blocked by: none

## Question

How should work beyond the initial wave and board approximation be represented without expanding the MVP?

## Resolution

Maintain a separate future physics roadmap for later investigations into breaking/reforming waves, higher fidelity board geometry and fin effects, rider mass/pose coupling, environmental conditions, detailed failure recording, and more complete board-water coupling. Breaking waves, whitewater, and overhangs are explicitly deferred from the first interactive-water feature. Basic water appearance, same-build seeded replay, board response, and MVP plausibility checks are required in the MVP; they are not deferred roadmap items. Wave-driven forward travel after a correctly timed pop-up is a hard current requirement and must emerge from board-water forces, never a timed translation or staged ride animation.
